26 /**
27  * @brief The surface precipitation rate of each level's step, for the force-restore
28  * hydrology (ERF_ForceRestoreHydrologyState.H) and snow layer
29  * (ERF_ForceRestoreSnowState.H), with erf.radiation.seb_precipitation_source.
30  *
31  * Owned by TwoStreamRadiation. Per level, and per step:
32  * - begin_step(...) at the start of ERF::Advance, before the dycore and microphysics,
33  * stores the surface precipitation accumulation of every column;
34  * - end_step(...) after microphysics turns its change since that start into the rate;
35  * - the hydrology and snow steps take the rate through rain(...) and frozen(...);
36  * - mark_used(...) after them closes the step.
37  * A rate is used once. When no step followed end_step (the balance skips a new level's
38  * first step), begin_step keeps the earlier start and the next end_step gives the mean rate
39  * over both steps; the scale of rain(...) and frozen(...) is then the time since that start
40  * over the step, so the carried precipitation is counted once, in full. When a step runs
41  * without a new end_step (a level whose radiation is interpolated has neither begin_step
42  * nor end_step), the scale is zero: the rate of an earlier step is not applied again.
43  *
44  * Without the snow layer the rate has one component, the total precipitation. With
45  * erf.radiation.seb_snow_enable it has two, the rain (Rain) and the frozen part (Frozen),
46  * filled in one pass over the accumulators. The rate needs no checkpoint or regrid
47  * handling: both captures happen inside one ERF::Advance of the level.
48  */
