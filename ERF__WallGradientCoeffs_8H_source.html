8 /**
9  * Coefficients of the one-sided z-gradient of a cell-centered velocity at a Dirichlet
10  * (no-slip) z wall,
11  *
12  * du/dz|wall = (c_wall * u_wall + c_0 * u_0 + c_1 * u_1) / dz0,
13  *
14  * where u_wall is the wall value, u_0 and u_1 are the first and second cells off the wall,
15  * and dz0 and dz1 are their heights. This is the stencil ComputeStrain_S and
16  * ComputeStrain_T use for the wall stress, with the same arithmetic; on a uniform mesh it
17  * reduces to (-8/3, 3, -1/3), the stencil of ComputeStrain_N. It is second order, so it
18  * is exact for a quadratic profile. The coefficients sum to zero.
19  *
20  * The implicit vertical momentum solve closes its wall row with this same gradient: the
21  * solve removes the explicit wall stress (from tau_corr) and puts back its own, so any
22  * other wall stencil changes the operator by implicit_fac * (L_solve - L_explicit) at the
23  * wall, and moves the steady state of a wall-bounded flow.
24  */

27  amrex::Real c_0;
28  amrex::Real c_1;
29 };
