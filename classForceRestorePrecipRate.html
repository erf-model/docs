Detailed Description

The surface precipitation rate of each level's step, for the force-restore hydrology (ERF_ForceRestoreHydrologyState.H) and snow layer (ERF_ForceRestoreSnowState.H), with erf.radiation.seb_precipitation_source.

Owned by TwoStreamRadiation. Per level, and per step:

  • begin_step(...) at the start of ERF::Advance, before the dycore and microphysics, stores the surface precipitation accumulation of every column;
  • end_step(...) after microphysics turns its change since that start into the rate;
  • the hydrology and snow steps take the rate through rain(...) and frozen(...);
  • mark_used(...) after them closes the step. A rate is used once. When no step followed end_step (the balance skips a new level's first step), begin_step keeps the earlier start and the next end_step gives the mean rate over both steps; the scale of rain(...) and frozen(...) is then the time since that start over the step, so the carried precipitation is counted once, in full. When a step runs without a new end_step (a level whose radiation is interpolated has neither begin_step nor end_step), the scale is zero: the rate of an earlier step is not applied again.

Without the snow layer the rate has one component, the total precipitation. With erf.radiation.seb_snow_enable it has two, the rain (Rain) and the frozen part (Frozen), filled in one pass over the accumulators. The rate needs no checkpoint or regrid handling: both captures happen inside one ERF::Advance of the level.
