Detailed Description

The per-level state of the force-restore snow layer (ERF_ForceRestoreSnow.H, erf.radiation.seb_snow_enable): the snow water equivalent and the running totals that close each column's snow budget.

Owned by TwoStreamRadiation, beside the force-restore hydrology (ERF_ForceRestoreHydrologyState.H) and the precipitation rate of the step (ERF_ForceRestorePrecipRate.H). step(...) after the balance, and before the hydrology step, applies one snow step to every open column: it takes the frozen precipitation, changes T_s where snow melts and leaves the water for the soil in soil_water(lev), which the hydrology step takes. The snow water equivalent and the totals are checkpointed, and TwoStreamRadiation lists them with its prognostic fields for regrid, coarse-fine filling and averaging down.
