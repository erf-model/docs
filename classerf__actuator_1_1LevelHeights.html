Detailed Description

mesh_z_levels of one level, kept between calls: the heights of a level change only when its boxes do (a regrid) or when the terrain moves, so a caller that needs the table at every step builds it once.

The table of z_phys_nd's level. Built at the first call, and again when z_phys_nd's boxes or geom's domain differ from those of the last build, or with rebuild true (for heights that change in time on the same boxes: a moving terrain). A collective when it builds; every rank builds at the same calls, as the boxes, the domain and rebuild are the same on every rank.
