Detailed Description

An ocean coupler's current at the cells of the surface: the eastward and northward components, the cosine and sine of the angle of the grid x axis from east, and the ocean's coverage, each on one 2D box at k = 0 for each box of the current, with one more lateral ghost cell than the current.
