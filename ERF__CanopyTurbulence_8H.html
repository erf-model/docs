Add the canopy TKE source to the RhoKE component of the staged cell source on one box. Called by make_sources inside its loop over the boxes, so it adds no pass over the level; cells outside the forest (f <= 0) return before the velocities are read. Fully immersed cells (terrain_blank = 1) get no source, where ERF_AdvanceDycore.cpp also zeroes the eddy diffusivities.
