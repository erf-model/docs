Copy a 2D field (k = 0 only) into the k = 0 plane of a surface-model field.

Surface-model fields keep their surface values in the k = 0 plane, which may lie in the field's z ghost region (SLM stores soil layers at k < 0), and carry x/y ghost cells the source need not have. Only the i,j extent of each destination VALID box is written, so ghost columns and every other k are left alone. The two fields must share a horizontal layout: the destination's boxes flattened to k = 0 equal the source's, on the same DistributionMapping – which holds for ERF's ba2d and a land model's data built from the same level grids, and is asserted rather than assumed.

The calendar sun of the two-stream model at a UTC epoch time (start_time + t).

The date as the RRTMGP interface forms it, the orbit of the year (or the erf.rad_orbital_* overrides) cached in orbit, and the declination and distance factor of ERF_OrbCosZenith.H. The two-stream sweep places its sun from this, with the column's position, and so does the immersed-boundary balance with erf.ibseb.sun_mode = two_stream.

The calendar sun of the two-stream model at a UTC epoch time: the calendar day with the time of day as its fraction, the declination and the Earth-Sun distance factor, from the date as the RRTMGP interface forms it and the orbital code of ERF_OrbCosZenith.H (orbital_params once per year into orbit, with the erf.rad_orbital_* overrides passed to it, then orbital_decl per call). set_solar_state() takes the sun of every sweep from here, and the immersed-boundary balance its erf.ibseb.sun_mode = two_stream sun, so the two cannot drift apart.
