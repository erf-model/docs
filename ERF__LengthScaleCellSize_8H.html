amrex::GpuArray<amrex::Real, AMREX_SPACEDIM> LengthScaleCellSize ( const amrex::Geometry &  geom)
inline

Cell sizes for formulas that use a lateral cell size as a length scale rather than to divide a difference: LES filter widths, scale-aware parameterizations, drag rates and per-area densities.

On a domain with one cell in y and more than one in x (an x-z run), dy is arbitrary, so dx is used in its place; likewise dy replaces dx on a domain with one cell in x. The formula then keeps its 3-D form, as if the unresolved direction had the spacing of the resolved one. A single column (one cell in both) keeps the dx and dy of the inputs as its nominal column size.

The level's own domain decides: a lateral direction with one cell is not refined (amr.ref_ratio_vect = r 1 r), so it has one cell on every level, and the spacing copied is that of the level.
