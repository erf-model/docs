Detailed Description

ERF's side of the structure coupling: the start-up and advance calls of the structure model, the check of its support against the finer level, and its source on the coarser levels with the checkpoint of that source.

The other calls (momentum sources, checkpoint, plotfile) sit where ERF does that work; see the call order in ERF_StructureModel.H.
