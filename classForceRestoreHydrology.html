Detailed Description

The per-level state of the force-restore hydrology (ERF_ForceRestoreHydrology.H): the running totals that close each column's water budget.

Owned by TwoStreamRadiation, which keeps the force-restore state itself (q_s, q_deep, T_deep) and the rain rate of the step (ERF_ForceRestorePrecipRate.H). step(...) at the force-restore update after the dycore applies one hydrology step to every open column and adds to the running totals. The totals are checkpointed, and TwoStreamRadiation lists them with its prognostic fields for regrid, coarse-fine filling and averaging down.
