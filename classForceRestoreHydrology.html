Detailed Description

The per-level state of the force-restore hydrology (ERF_ForceRestoreHydrology.H): the rain rate of the current step, and the running totals that close each column's water budget.

Owned by TwoStreamRadiation, which keeps the force-restore state itself (q_s, q_deep, T_deep). Per level, and per step:

  • begin_step(...) at the start of ERF::Advance, before the dycore and microphysics, stores the surface precipitation accumulation of every column;
  • end_step(...) after microphysics turns its change over the step into the rain rate;
  • step(...) at the force-restore update after the dycore applies one hydrology step to every open column and adds to the running totals. The rain rate needs no checkpoint or regrid handling: both captures happen inside one ERF::Advance of the level. The totals are checkpointed, and TwoStreamRadiation lists them with its prognostic fields for regrid, coarse-fine filling and averaging down.
