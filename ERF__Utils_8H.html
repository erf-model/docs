Average down a cell-centred state over immersed-forcing levels (erf.if_average_down = fluid_mass). The fine cells are weighted by their fluid mass, (rho S)_c = rho_c * sum (1 - beta_f) V_f (rho S)_f / sum (1 - beta_f) V_f rho_f, with beta the solid fraction, V the fine cell volume (detJ / (m_x m_y), the weight the plain average carries in the state) and rho_c the coarse density: the fluid-volume-weighted mean of the fine density, sum (1 - beta_f) V_f rho_f / sum (1 - beta_f) V_f, when the density is in the component range, else the coarse cell's own. The coarse value is the fluid mean over the whole coarse cell (intensive), not the sum over the fine cells. Coarse cells that are solid on their own level, or with no fluid fine cell under them, keep their value. The plain average mixes the values of solid fine cells into coarse fluid cells.
