Detailed Description

What the two-stream sweep hands the building faces of the immersed-boundary surface energy balance (erf.ibseb.radiation = two_stream), and how a face reads it.

The sweep already writes the interface fluxes of every column into ERF's rad_fluxes (RRTMGP's level layout: index k the lower interface of layer k, component 0 SW up, 1 SW down, 2 LW up, 3 LW down, after the clear/cloudy blend). What it does not keep is the direct beam, which only its scratch holds, and the cosine of the zenith it used. On the levels where ERF::init_ibseb() asks for them (TwoStreamRadiation::supply_canopy_forcing()) every sweep also writes:

  • the direct beam on a horizontal surface at the interfaces m = 0 .. m_top of every column, counted from the surface, m_top the highest interface a face of the level reads (a field on the level's grids flattened in z and then given those interfaces, index k = m), blended clear/cloudy like the fluxes;
  • the cosine of the zenith angle, floored at zero (a 2D field). A face reads the column of its fluid cell at its own height (two_stream_canopy_sample()): the downwelling fluxes there are the sun and the sky that reach it (for height-map buildings no building stands above a face in its own column; a face under the overhang of an STL building sees only building in IBFaceSet's view fractions, which take each column as solid up to its top, so its column's fluxes carry no weight), and the upwelling fluxes there are the ground as seen from that height, through the air in between.

Write the direct beam on a horizontal surface at the interfaces 0 .. m_top of column (i,j), from the scratch of the evaluation that has just run there, into beam: the clear-sky evaluation's beam as it is, and the cloudy evaluation's blended in with the cloud fraction, by the same expression as the fluxes ((1 - f) clear + f cloudy), so the two round alike in single precision and the shortwave down less the beam is the diffuse light.

vertical_two_stream_sweep() keeps the Beer-Lambert beam of every interface in its scratch (TwoStreamScratch::F_DIR), but writes it only when the shortwave is on and the sun is up; otherwise the scratch holds the other evaluation's beam or nothing at all, and the beam is zero. Call it right after each evaluation, before the next one overwrites the scratch.
