int  level,

The weight with which the fluxes of RK stage nrk at this level go into the flux registers: the weight of that stage's fluxes in the update the step ends with, or zero if they are not registered. Only with two-way coupling and more than one level. The compressible integrator and the anelastic MidPoint integrator update from their final stage alone (nrk = 2 and nrk = 1), which takes weight 1. The anelastic RK2 integrator is Heun's, S^{n+1} = S^n + dt (R0 + R1) / 2, so both of its stages take weight 1/2. Every stage that adds fluxes to the registers must use this, so that the registers hold the fluxes the update applied.
