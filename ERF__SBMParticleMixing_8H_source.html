17 /**
18  * Turbulent gradient mixing of spectral particles. One coefficient acts on
19  * every member of every bin group, and particle molecular (Brownian)
20  * diffusion is zero under every policy.
21  *
22  * HostPassiveScalar consumes ERF's Scalar_h/Scalar_v eddy channels, which are
23  * already density weighted and Schmidt scaled. Under ERF's LES closures they
24  * equal the Q channels that diffuse moisture, but the PBL schemes other than
25  * SHOC fill Q_v and not Scalar_v, so this policy is for LES closures only and
26  * enabling it must reject a PBL scheme. ConstantReference multiplies
27  * kinematic reference diffusivities by the face-averaged dry-air density.
28  */
29 enum class ParticleMixingPolicy { None, ConstantReference, HostPassiveScalar };
