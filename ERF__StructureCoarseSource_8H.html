Detailed Description

The restriction of a structure model's momentum source onto the coarser levels.

ERF::make_structure_coarse_sources calls it at the start of each step of a level below the model's level (erf.coupling_type = TwoWay). The restriction keeps the force the source puts on the air, the integral of the source over the faces' control volumes, on uniform, stretched and terrain-following meshes.
