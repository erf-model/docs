Detailed Description

Low-overhead Stage 1 conserved-scalar budget accumulator.

Fluxes are positive in the positive coordinate direction. The reported boundary balance is therefore low-minus-high for each coordinate pair. Local face sums are held until a configured report interval, then one packed MPI reduction produces the six-face values. For the anelastic RK2 path, nrk=0 is retained and nrk=1 applies the trapezoidal stage weight; this avoids counting both RHS evaluations as two physical timesteps.
