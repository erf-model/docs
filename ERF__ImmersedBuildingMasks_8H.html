Detailed Description

How the buildings immersed forcing reads a momentum face and which forcing it applies there, shared by the source kernels (ImmersedForcingBuildings_{X,Y,Z}mom) and the implicit projection (ERF::make_if_projection_sigma, erf.if_implicit_projection, erf-model/ERF#4887), so the two agree face by face on the blanking, on which faces carry the MOST wall law and on which the linear drag toward zero.
