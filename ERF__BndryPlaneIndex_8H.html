The index along a boundary plane that fills the ghost cell at index i: inside the domain, i itself; outside it, the periodic image when the direction is periodic, so that a ghost cell beside the inflow face holds what the plane holds at the periodic image (as the interior cells across the seam do); else the nearest cell of the plane. A plane holds the domain's cells only along its face, so its ghost cells in the other directions come from here.
