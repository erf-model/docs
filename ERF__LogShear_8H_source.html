14 /**
15  * erf.rans_shear_stencil = log_law (k-eqn RANS, issue 5261): the vertical gradients of u and v in
16  * the strain invariant S_mn S_mn, exact for a logarithmic profile over the ground.
17  *
18  * ComputeSmnSmn averages the strains of the faces below and above a cell, which for du/dz is the
19  * central difference (u_{k+1} - u_{k-1}) / (z_{k+1} - z_{k-1}) on uniform layers. For the profile
20  * u = (u_* / kappa) ln((d + z0) / z0) that difference is larger than du/dz at the cell center by
21  * the factor (d_k + z0) ln((d_{k+1} + z0) / (d_{k-1} + z0)) / (d_{k+1} - d_{k-1}): 1.21 in the
22  * second cell of uniform layers, 1.06 in the third and 1.03 in the fourth (z0 << dz), and
23  * 1 + dz^2 / (3 d^2) far from the ground. The shear production of k, 2 nu_t S_mn S_mn, is then
24  * 46 %, 12 % and 6 % high there (45 %, 12 % and 6 % with z0 = 0.1 m and dz = 15.625 m).
25  *
26  * Here, in a cell k above the wall cell and below the top cell,
27  *
28  * du/dz = (u_{k+1} - u_{k-1}) / [ (d_k + z0) ln((d_{k+1} + z0) / (d_{k-1} + z0)) ]
29  *
30  * and the same for v, with u and v averaged to the cell centers, d_k the closure's wall distance,
31  * d_{k-1} and d_{k+1} that distance less and plus the height differences of the cell centers, and
32  * z0 the surface layer's roughness length of the column. S_mn S_mn changes by the difference of
33  * its (1,3) and (2,3) terms; dw/dx, dw/dy and the other components stay as ComputeStrain_* formed
34  * them. A cell whose d_{k-1} + z0 would not be positive is left as it is.
35  *
36  * The stencil is exact for the log profile only. For a profile that is linear in d it returns the
37  * gradient divided by the factor above (17 %, 6 % and 3 % low in the cells 2, 3 and 4), so it is
38  * less accurate than the central difference where the profile is closer to linear than to
39  * logarithmic: in a stable layer above about L / 5 (L the Obukhov length), and well above the
40  * ground, where both errors are below 1 %. On sloping ground d is the distance to the wall and
41  * the height differences are vertical, so the form is exact to leading order in the slope only.
42  */

123  // distance as it is.
