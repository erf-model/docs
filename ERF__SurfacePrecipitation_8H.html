Total liquid-water-equivalent surface precipitation accumulation [kg/m2] of every column, from the scheme-native accumulators.

Fills accum_2d, a one-component MultiFab on the level's boxes coarsened to one cell in z (box n of accum_2d is box n of the accumulators, with the same owner), from the k = klo plane of the sources. Schemes that provide a total give it directly; schemes that provide the species give their sum, so nothing is counted twice (the same rule as the 2D plotfile precipitation diagnostics). Without any source the field is zero. A box whose 3D box does not hold the k = klo plane (a fine level that does not span z, or the upper box of a column split in z) has no ground to read and stays zero, so no accumulator is read outside its box. Used by the fire layer's fuel moisture (ERF_FirePrecip.H) and the force-restore hydrology (ERF_ForceRestoreHydrology.H).
