Total liquid-water-equivalent surface precipitation accumulation [kg/m2] of every column, from the scheme-native accumulators.

Fills component 0 of accum_2d, a MultiFab on the level's boxes coarsened to one cell in z (box n of accum_2d is box n of the accumulators, with the same owner), from the k = klo plane of the sources. Schemes that provide a total give it directly; schemes that provide the species give their sum, so nothing is counted twice (the same rule as the 2D plotfile precipitation diagnostics). Without any source the field is zero. A box whose 3D box does not hold the k = klo plane (a fine level that does not span z, or the upper box of a column split in z) has no ground to read and stays zero, so no accumulator is read outside its box. Used by the fire layer's fuel moisture (ERF_FirePrecip.H) and the force-restore precipitation rate (ERF_ForceRestorePrecipRate.H).

With phase = Frozen the field is the frozen part, snow + graupel + hail (zero for a scheme without those accumulators). With phase = Liquid it is the rest: the total minus the frozen part, or without a total the rain accumulator. Frozen plus Liquid is then the Total field for every scheme with a total, including one whose total holds more than its species (Morrison's also counts the cloud water and cloud ice that fell out). The Liquid field is not bounded at zero: where the frozen accumulators exceed the total by round-off it is slightly negative, and a caller that takes the change over a step bounds that change (force_restore_rain_rate).
