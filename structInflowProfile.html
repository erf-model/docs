Detailed Description

Terrain-following inflow profiles for Inflow and InflowOutflow faces (xlo.inflow_profile = "log_law" or "file").

A profile tabulates u, v and, when given, theta and tke against height above the local ground. Each boundary cell looks the profile up at its own height above the terrain beneath it, so the inflow follows the terrain along a sloping face, as WindNinja's inlets do. The terrain on the boundary is left as it is.
