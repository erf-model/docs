Detailed Description

2D horizontal flood grid

A slab one cell thick with C*nx x C*ny cells, where C is erf.flood.grid_ratio and (nx, ny) is the size of the region the atmospheric level's boxes cover: the whole domain on level 0, the refined region on a finer level.
