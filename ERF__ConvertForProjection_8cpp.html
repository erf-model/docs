Face weights beta = den_mlt / den_div mapped onto the faces the way the advection scheme maps a scalar onto them: the cell-centered ratio den_mlt / den_div (on the valid and ghost cells) is reconstructed on each face by BuildScalarAdvectionFaceValues with the given schemes and upwind fractions, the upwind side picked from the sign of the momenta passed in. On a pseudo-incompressible level, with den_mlt = rho0 theta0 and den_div = rho, this is the face value of theta with which (rho theta) is advected when rho theta = rho0 theta0, so the projected flux beta (rho u) is the advective flux of (rho theta).

Parameters
[in]den_divdensity divided out, at cell centers (component Rho_comp), with ghost cells
[in]den_mltdensity multiplied in, at cell centers (component Rho_comp), with ghost cells
[in]xmomx-face momentum (its sign picks the upwind side)
[in]ymomy-face momentum (its sign picks the upwind side)
[in]zmomz-face vertical mass flux, Omega on a terrain-fitted mesh (its sign picks the upwind side)
[out]betaface weights on the valid faces of xmom, ymom and zmom (defined here)
[in]horiz_adv_typehorizontal advection scheme
[in]vert_adv_typevertical advection scheme
[in]horiz_upw_frachorizontal upwind fraction
[in]vert_upw_fracvertical upwind fraction
[in]periodperiodicity of the level, to fill the ratio's ghost cells between boxes

The ratio is computed on the valid and ghost cells and then its ghost cells inside the domain (between boxes and across periodic boundaries) are filled from the neighbouring valid cells, so that they hold the same values as the valid cells there: the reconstruction reads two or three cells past the face, and the base state's ghost cells beyond the first are not filled between boxes. Outside the domain the ratio comes from the ghost cells of den_div and den_mlt; a cell with no density (covered by an embedded boundary, or a corner the boundary conditions leave unfilled) gets the ratio one.

Face weights beta = den_mlt / den_div with which ConvertForProjection converts (den_div u) to (den_mlt u), on the valid faces of xmom, ymom and zmom: the same face averages and the same one-sided values on the ext_dir and ext_dir_upwind faces of the lateral domain boundaries (the upwind side chosen from the sign of the momentum passed in). Multiplying the momenta by beta is then what ConvertForProjection does, and dividing by the same beta undoes it.

Parameters
[in]den_divdensity divided out, at cell centers (component Rho_comp)

[in]ymomy-face momentum (its sign picks the upwind side)

[out]betaface weights on the valid faces of xmom, ymom and zmom (defined here)
[in]domainDomain at this level
[in]domain_bcs_type_hhost vector for domain boundary conditions

A face with no density on either side (covered by an embedded boundary) gets beta = 1.
