Add sign * (sum over m = k..khi of w(m)) to sed(i,j,k,n) for every k in [klo, khi].

Called with sign = +1 and w(m) = rho*q*dz before a sedimentation step and with sign = -1 and the same w after it, this adds to each face k the mass that crossed it downward: what the cells above it lost. The face above khi gets nothing (no inflow).
