Detailed Description

Transactional owner for one SBM coarse/fine interface.

Accepted chunk-local mapped stage rates are added directly to YA using the stage's completed-step time coefficient. The register is only refluxable after the coarse step and every expected fine substep have been accepted. The authoritative spectrum and the mapped candidate remain externally owned.
