4 /**
5  * @file ERF_SEBTurbulentFlux.H
6  * @brief Where the simplified surface energy balance takes its sensible (H) and
7  * latent (LE) heat fluxes from.
8  *
9  * The force-restore update removes H + LE from the ground. The air above receives
10  * the flux the lower surface layer applies, which it computes from the same skin
11  * temperature. Unless the two are the same flux the ground never loses what the
12  * air gains, so by default the balance takes the surface layer's flux.
13  *
14  * Precedence, per field and per level:
15  * 1. a land-surface model field of that name ("hfx" or "lh");
16  * 2. the surface layer's applied flux, with seb_turbulent_flux_source =
17  * surface_layer and the flux field allocated: a zlo surface layer with any
18  * diffusion or closure, not on EB terrain; the latent flux only with a
19  * moisture model. An adiabatic surface layer has the field and a zero flux,
20  * so H (or LE) is then 0, and ERF warns at start-up if the scalar default it
21  * replaces is nonzero;
22  * 3. the scalar default (seb_hfx_default or seb_lh_default).
23  *
24  * The surface-layer flux is the conservative flux on the lowest z face (rho
25  * w'theta' and rho w'qv'), converted to W/m^2 by the same helpers as the
26  * sensible_heat_flux and latent_heat_flux 2D outputs, so the two cannot
27  * disagree. Positive is away from the surface, as in the balance.
28  */
