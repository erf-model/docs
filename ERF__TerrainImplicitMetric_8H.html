Terrain-metric part of the vertical diffusion on a terrain-fitted mesh, for erf.implicit_terrain_metric.

The flux through a zeta face projects the horizontal fluxes onto the face normal. Their h*d/dz parts add a vertical-diffusion term K_h * M * d/dz to the zeta flux, with the factors below. The explicit operator forms it from averaged horizontal fluxes; with the option on, the same term in compact form (face differences in zeta) is also put into the implicit vertical solve and subtracted explicitly at the state the explicit terms see, so the stages with an implicit solve treat the K_h * M * d2/dz2 stiffness implicitly. The factors are the exact du/dz (dphi/dz) content of the projected fluxes, so for smooth fields on a uniform slope the explicit remainder (averaged minus compact) is small; at the grid scale it is the whole compact term with the opposite sign, cancelled within the stage by the implicit solve, which is why every stage needs the solve (SolverChoice::check_params).

h_xi and h_eta are the terrain slopes dz/dxi and dz/deta at the face, mx and my the map factors there. Momentum: the zeta flux of u holds -0.5 * mu_h_tot * M_u * du/dz with M_u = (4/3) (h_xi mx)^2 + (h_eta my)^2, from h_xi (S11 - er/3) and h_eta S12: S11 carries -h_xi mx du/dz, and the expansion rate er carries the same term through Omega, so the deviatoric S11 - er/3 carries -(2/3) h_xi mx du/dz. For v, M_v = (h_xi mx)^2 + (4/3) (h_eta my)^2, from h_xi S12 and h_eta (S22 - er/3).
