Detailed Description

Water of the two-stream force-restore surface: rain in, runoff, a prognostic deep reservoir with gravitational drainage, and a prognostic deep temperature.

The force-restore balance (ERF_SimplifiedSEB.H) carries q_s, the volumetric water content [m^3/m^3] of a surface layer d_s deep (erf.radiation.seb_moisture_layer_depth_m), restored toward q_deep over tau_q. These functions add the terms of Noilhan and Planton (1989, "NP89") and Mahfouf and Noilhan (1996, "MN96") that it leaves out, each behind its own input:

  • Rain (erf.radiation.seb_precipitation_source = microphysics). P [kg/m^2/s] enters the surface layer as in NP89 Eq. (11) and Deardorff (1978) Eq. (12), with depth d_1 = d_s: dq_s/dt += P / (rho_w d_s). NP89 Eq. (11) multiplies (P - E) by one coefficient C1. The existing balance takes E out of q_s with C1 = 1 (prognostic_dqs_dt), so rain uses C1 = 1 too. NP89 Eq. (19) and Table 2 give C1 = 0.08-0.34 at saturation, rising as the soil dries, and D78 Eq. (13) gives C1 = 0.5-14. With C1 = 1 the 10 cm layer fills faster than with NP89's C1 near saturation, so heavy rain runs off sooner than in NP89.
  • Runoff (with rain). Water above the porosity w_sat runs off (NP89 Sec. 4a: "Runoff occurs when w_g or w_2 exceeds the saturation value w_sat"). The surface layer's runoff that leaves the column is at most this step's rain: water above w_sat that was already in q_s (a restore overshoot, or a restart from a run without hydrology) is clamped off q_s and does not come out of the column.
  • Deep reservoir (erf.radiation.seb_deep_prognostic_enable). q_deep becomes w_2, the mean water content of the soil column of depth d_2 (erf.radiation.seb_deep_layer_depth_m), which contains the surface layer (NP89 Eq. 12, MN96 Eq. 4): dq_deep/dt = (P - E) / (rho_w d_2). Since w_2 is the mean of the whole column, the restore term of q_s moves water within the column and does not appear here; the column's water is rho_w d_2 q_deep.
  • Deep temperature (with the deep reservoir). T_2 relaxes toward T_s over tau (erf.radiation.seb_restore_timescale_s), NP89 Eq. (6): dT_2/dt = (T_s - T_2) / tau.
  • Gravitational drainage (erf.radiation.seb_drainage_enable). Water above field capacity w_fc leaves the bottom of the column, MN96 Eq. (4): dq_deep/dt += -(C_3 / tau_1d) max(0, q_deep - w_fc), with C_3 = (C_3 d_2) / d_2 (MN96 Eq. 16 and Table 1, which tabulates C_3 for d_2 = 1 m). MN96 Eq. (15) makes C_3 proportional to tau, and Table 1 is computed with tau = 1 day (MN96 appendix), so the drainage uses tau_1d = 86400 s, not seb_restore_timescale_s. The drainage rate then does not depend on that input.

The relaxation terms (drainage, deep temperature) are linear in the state, so each is integrated exactly over the step, x_new - x_eq = (x_old - x_eq) exp(-k dt); the update cannot overshoot whatever the step. The source terms (rain, evaporation) are forward Euler, as the existing balance is.

References:

  • Deardorff, J. W., 1978: Efficient prediction of ground surface temperature and moisture, with inclusion of a layer of vegetation. J. Geophys. Res., 83, 1889-1903.
  • Noilhan, J., and S. Planton, 1989: A simple parameterization of land surface processes for meteorological models. Mon. Wea. Rev., 117, 536-549.
  • Mahfouf, J.-F., and J. Noilhan, 1996: Inclusion of gravitational drainage in a land surface scheme based on the force-restore method. J. Appl. Meteor., 35, 987-992.

One step of the force-restore hydrology for one column, after the balance has updated T_s and q_s (prognostic_dTs_dt, prognostic_dqs_dt).

In order: rain into the surface layer and the surface runoff above the porosity (at most the step's rain: water that never infiltrates); the infiltrated rain minus the evaporation into the column, then its drainage; the deep temperature toward the updated T_s; runoff of the column above the porosity; the bounds of q_s and q_deep. The surface runoff is taken before the drainage, so water that never entered the soil does not drain.

With the deep reservoir, the column then satisfies exactly rho_w d_2 (q_deep_new - q_deep_old) = (P - E) dt - drainage - runoff + deficit.
