14 /**
15  * Spread point forces onto the face-centred momentum sources with a 3-D Gaussian kernel.
16  *
17  * Each point p carries a force F_p on the fluid (N) and has its own kernel width epsilon_p
18  * (the conductor lines and the towers of a run may differ). Its share of the source at a face is
19  * F_p w_p(face) / S_p, where w_p = exp(-|x_face - x_p|^2 / epsilon_p^2) is cut off beyond
20  * 3 epsilon_p and S_p = sum over the faces of that component of w_p dV is the discrete
21  * normalisation with the face volumes (dx dy dz detJ on a terrain-following mesh). The sum
22  * of src dV over the mesh therefore equals F_p exactly for every point and component,
23  * whatever the resolution and however much of the kernel is cut off by the ground or the
24  * domain top; the kernel's shape is Gaussian only where it is resolved. The faces sit at
25  * prob_lo + i dx with the volumes dx dy dz detJ, with no map factors: a body model must refuse a
26  * run whose map factors are not 1 until the core carries them. The normalisation sums are
27  * accumulated with atomic adds, so on a GPU the last bits of the source can differ between runs;
28  * CPU runs are bitwise reproducible.
29  *
30  * Every rank accumulates the faces its boxes own (each physical face once, however the boxes
31  * share their boundary faces) and the normalisations are summed across ranks, so the result
32  * does not depend on the domain decomposition. Every periodic image of a point within
33  * 3 epsilon_p of the domain (in x and y; z is not imaged) adds its kernel, so a kernel wraps across
34  * a periodic boundary, and overlaps itself when the period is under 6 epsilon_p. The sources are
35  * set (not added to) on every valid face, both copies of a shared face alike; the faces on a
36  * non-periodic domain boundary get no source.
37  *
38  * Aborts when a position or force is not finite. z_phys_nd and detJ_cc must share the boxes and
39  * distribution of src_x; src_x, src_y and src_z are face-centred in x, y and z.
40  *
41  * @param[in] pos 3*n point coordinates (m, ERF domain coordinates)
42  * @param[in] force 3*n forces ON the fluid BY the body (N, ERF frame): minus the fluid's force on the body
43  * @param[in] epsilon n kernel widths, one per point (m, > 0 and finite)
44  * @param[in] z_phys_nd nodal heights (m), or nullptr on a uniform-dz mesh
45  * @param[in] detJ_cc cell Jacobians (1 ghost cell), or nullptr on a uniform-dz mesh
46  * @param[in] geom the level's geometry
47  * @param[out] src_x,src_y,src_z face-centred momentum sources (N/m^3), one component each
48  */
