25 /**
26  * @brief Restrict face-centred momentum sources from level L down to level lev < L
27  *
28  * src holds the source [N/m^3] on the faces of grids[L] (x, y, z faces; it may have ghost
29  * faces). On return it holds the source on the faces of grids[lev], without ghost faces,
30  * zero away from the region level L covers.
31  *
32  * The force on a face is the source times the face's control volume, J_f dx dy dz, with
33  * J_f the mesh Jacobian on the face (the mean of detJ in the two cells beside it, the one
34  * cell inside the domain on a non-periodic domain face). The restriction goes down one level
35  * at a time: coarse face I takes the forces of the fine faces r I + m, |m| < r, along the
36  * face normal with the weights (r - |m|) / r, and of every fine face across it. Each fine
37  * force is shared out with weights that add up to 1, so the total force is kept exactly,
38  * also when the fine level is split into boxes or crosses a periodic boundary. On a
39  * non-periodic domain face, where the boundary condition sets the momentum, a fine face
40  * inside the domain gives its whole force to the first coarse face inside, so the force
41  * on the faces inside the domain is kept too. The coarse source is the coarse force divided
42  * by the coarse face's control volume.
43  *
44  * detJ[l] is detJ_cc of level l, with one ghost cell filled, on a stretched or
45  * terrain-following mesh, and nullptr on a uniform-dz mesh (J_f = 1 there, and no Jacobian
46  * is applied).
47  */
