Height above the ground of the horizontal face kf at the centre of column (i,j). With z_phys_nd (zp) it is the mean of the four nodes of the face minus that of the bottom face klo; without it, the uniform spacing dz above the domain bottom. Wind farm hub heights are measured from the ground, as in WindFarm::fill_SMark_multifab*.
