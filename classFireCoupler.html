Inject the lagged fire heat, vapour and smoke into the cell sources.

Called from the slow right-hand side at every Runge-Kutta stage on the fire grid's level, after make_sources has rebuilt cc_source. The lagged fluxes are fixed over the step, so every stage receives the same tendency (erf.fire.source_mode decides whether it replaces or adds to the theta and vapour sources). Advance the fire one step of its level and refill the lagged fluxes.

Called from ERF::Advance() once per step of the fire grid's level, after that level's dycore. The air temperature and humidity at k = 0 come from the state before the dycore; the wind from before the dycore (passive and lagged coupling) or after it (synchronous).
