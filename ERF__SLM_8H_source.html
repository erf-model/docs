170 /**
171  * The Simplified Land-surface Model (SLM) supplies lower-boundary
172  * sensible-heat, latent-heat, and momentum fluxes for land cells.
173  *
174  * The original formulation and evaluation of SLM are documented in Lee and
175  * Khairoutdinov (2015), "A Simplified Land Model (SLM) for use in
176  * cloud-resolving models: Formulation and evaluation," Journal of Advances
177  * in Modeling Earth Systems, 7, 1368--1392,
178  * https://doi.org/10.1002/2014MS000419.
179  *
180  * SLM includes selected formulations adapted from NoahMP, updates inherited
181  * from gSAM-SLM, and SLM-specific corrections to the surface energy and water
182  * budgets. It remains a simplified land model and is not a complete
183  * implementation of NoahMP.
184  */
