static void ProblemBase::make_terrain_surface_periodic ( const amrex::Geometry &  geom,
amrex::FArrayBox &  terrain_fab 
)
inlinestatic

Give every node in a periodic direction the height of its periodic image inside the domain. The nodes on the high side of a periodic domain are the same nodes as those on the low side, but none of the sources above knows that: the file readers clip a node outside the table to its edge (and look every node up 1e-3 past where it is), and an analytic surface is periodic only to round-off. Left as they are the two copies of a node differ, and so do the metrics of the faces on either side of it – which means the two grids that share a face update it differently, the mass flux through it is no longer the same on both sides, and which copy a fill keeps depends on how the grids are distributed. The low side is the one we keep.

terrain_fab must hold every node of the domain in a periodic direction, since that is where the images are read from. A surface that is far from periodic is still made periodic here, but we say so, since the cells next to the high side then see a step.
