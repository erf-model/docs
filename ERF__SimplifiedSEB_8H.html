Detailed Description

Simplified SEB – the two-stream model's surface energy balance, as pure functions.

The residual diagnosis below, the force-restore tendencies of the skin temperature and soil water (prognostic_dTs_dt, prognostic_dqs_dt), the soil's moisture availability (seb_moisture_availability), the canopy and bare-soil resistances to evaporation (seb_canopy_resistance_without_vpd, seb_soil_evaporation_resistance), the relative humidity of bare soil's pore air (seb_soil_surface_relative_humidity), and the soil's thermal properties (seb_soil_heat_capacity, seb_soil_thermal_conductivity, seb_force_restore_heat_capacity).

Surface Energy Balance Equation: R_net(i,j) = SW_net(i,j) + LW_net(i,j) = [sw_flux_sfc(i,j)] + [lw_flux_sfc(i,j)]

SEB_residual(i,j) = R_net(i,j) - hfx_sfc(i,j) - lh_sfc(i,j) - grdflx_sfc(i,j)

Where:

  • sw_flux_sfc: net shortwave flux at surface [W/m^2]
  • lw_flux_sfc: net longwave flux at surface [W/m^2]
  • hfx_sfc: sensible heat flux (H) [W/m^2]
  • lh_sfc: latent heat flux (LE) [W/m^2]
  • grdflx_sfc: ground heat flux (G) [W/m^2]

A perfectly closed budget gives SEB_residual = 0; non-zero residual indicates energy not accounted for by the four main SEB terms.

References:

  • Oke, T. R., 1987: Boundary Layer Climates (2nd ed.), Routledge.

Canopy resistance to transpiration [s/m] without its vapour-deficit factor.

Noah's big-leaf Jarvis form (Chen et al. 1996) on the parameters and floors of Noah-MP's canopy-resistance option 2 (ResistanceCanopyStomataJarvisMod). Noah-MP itself applies the factors per sunlit and shaded leaf, with the absorbed PAR (f = 2 PAR / RGL), the canopy temperature and canopy-air humidity, and a root-zone soil-water factor; here one big leaf takes the shortwave reaching the surface, the skin temperature, the air at the surface layer's reference height and the top layer's soil-water factor:

r_c = RS / (LAI * F_sw * F_T * F_vpd * F_soil), F_sw = (f + RS/RSMAX) / (1 + f), f = 0.55 * 2 SW_down / (RGL * LAI), F_T = 1 - 0.0016 (TOPT - T)^2,

F_soil the soil-water factor (seb_moisture_availability) and F_vpd the vapour-deficit factor, which needs the air's humidity and is applied by the surface layer (erf_surface_moisture::vapour_deficit_factor); this returns r_c * F_vpd. F_sw and F_T are floored at 1e-4 as in Noah-MP, F_soil at 1e-6 (Noah-MP's MinThr).

Returns
r_c * F_vpd [s/m], capped at 1e6 (no transpiration), which is also the value without leaves or for a non-finite input. The surface layer divides by F_vpd and caps the result at 1e6 again (erf_surface_moisture::canopy_resistance).

Moisture availability beta of the force-restore surface, from its water content.

With the prognostic balance, q_s is the volumetric water content [m^3/m^3] of a surface layer seb_moisture_layer_depth_m deep (see prognostic_dqs_dt). The surface evaporates at the potential rate when that layer is at or above field capacity, not at all at or below the wilting point, and in proportion in between:

beta = clamp((q_s - q_wilt) / (q_fc - q_wilt), 0, 1)

the "beta" closure of bucket and force-restore land surfaces. The surface layer then takes the surface mixing ratio beta * q_sat(T_s, p_s) + (1 - beta) * q_air (erf_surface_moisture::surface_mixing_ratio), so its moisture flux is beta times the potential one.
