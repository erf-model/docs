Detailed Description

Inputs of the canopy terms of the k-eqn RANS model (erf.forest_canopy_*). The terms themselves are in Source/SourceTerms/ERF_CanopyTurbulence.H.

The canopy terms act on the forest drag field and on the k-eqn RANS model only: abort when either is missing, rather than ignore the input, and when the length cap would meet the explicit TKE dissipation.
