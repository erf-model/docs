Detailed Description

Where a fine index sits among the level-0 plane indices along one tangential direction: the two plane indices to blend and the weight on the second. The plane holds cell-centred values in every direction (the writer averages the face velocities to cell centres), so a cell-centred index and a nodal (face) index are both located by their positions relative to the plane's cell centres. At level 0 (ratio 1) a cell-centred index is the plane cell itself with zero weight on its neighbour, and node j is the average of plane cells j - 1 and j, the two cells it separates. Both indices are brought into the plane's extent, which is the level-0 domain: along a periodic direction they take the periodic image, as the interior cells across the seam do (bndry_plane_index); otherwise they are clamped.
