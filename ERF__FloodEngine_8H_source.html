13 /**
14  * @file ERF_FloodEngine.H
15  * @brief The interface between the flood coupling and a flood engine
16  *
17  * A flood engine is a surface-water model (shallow water, a runoff or routing
18  * model) that runs on the flood grid and takes the atmosphere's surface
19  * precipitation as its source. The shared coupling layer (FloodCoupler) owns
20  * everything that touches the atmosphere: the flood grid's placement on one
21  * level, the precipitation of each step taken from the microphysics
22  * accumulators, the wind handed to the engine, the engine's fields in the ERF
23  * plotfile and the check that a restart uses the same flood grid. An engine
24  * sees only this interface, FloodForcing and the helpers of ERF_FloodGrid.H.
25  *
26  * The flood coupling is one-way in this version: the engine reads the
27  * atmosphere and returns nothing to it.
28  *
29  * ERF itself contains no engine. An engine lives in its own directory outside
30  * ERF (FLOOD_HOME in GNU make, ERF_FLOOD_ENGINE_DIRS in CMake) and registers
31  * itself with ERF_REGISTER_FLOOD_ENGINE (ERF_FloodEngineRegistry.H).
32  * ERF_FloodEngineTemplate.H declares every method below for new engines: a
33  * method added to FloodEngine is declared there too (a pure virtual one that
34  * is missing fails the build; an optional one does not).
35  *
36  * Memory contract. Every MultiFab the coupling hands an engine (FloodForcing,
37  * the FloodGrid layout) and every one the engine hands back (plot_var) is an
38  * ordinary AMReX MultiFab in the default arena, so on a GPU build its data is
39  * on the device. An engine that runs on the host:
40  * - copies what it reads from FloodForcing to the host inside advance(),
41  * because those fields are temporaries of that call, and synchronizes the
42  * GPU stream (amrex::Gpu::streamSynchronize()) before it reads them there;
43  * - owns the MultiFabs it returns, allocated on fg.ba and fg.dm, keeps them
44  * alive until its next advance(), and copies them back to device memory
45  * before returning them.
46  * The coupling never frees, resizes or keeps across steps a pointer an engine
47  * returns.
48  */
49 
50 /**
51  * @brief The atmospheric state a flood engine reads for one step
52  *
53  * Every field lives on the level the flood grid refines
54  * (erf.flood.anchor_level), in that level's index space; the engine maps them
55  * onto its own grid (FloodGrid::atm_lo gives the corner of the flood region,
56  * fill_flood_from_atm_k0 copies a column field onto the flood grid). The k = 0
57  * slabs hold one cell in z per box of the level. The slabs are temporaries of
58  * FloodCoupler::advance(): an engine reads them during its advance() and keeps
59  * no pointer to them.
60  */
61 struct FloodForcing
62 {
63  double time; ///< Start of the step [s], in double as ERF's own clock
64  amrex::Real dt; ///< Step of the flood grid's level [s]
65  const amrex::MultiFab& xvel; ///< x-velocity on x-faces [m/s], after this step's dycore
66  const amrex::MultiFab& yvel; ///< y-velocity on y-faces [m/s], after this step's dycore
67  const amrex::MultiFab& z_phys_cc; ///< Cell-center height [m] of the terrain-following mesh
68  /**
69  * Surface precipitation of this step on the k = 0 slabs [kg/m2, equal to
70  * mm of liquid water]: component 0 the liquid part, component 1 the frozen
71  * part (snow, graupel and hail as liquid-water equivalent; Morrison and
72  * WSM6 count their cloud-water fallout, and Morrison its cloud-ice
73  * fallout, as liquid). It is the change of the microphysics accumulators
74  * over the step, so the sum over the steps of a run is the change of ERF's
75  * own accumulation over the run. Not bounded at zero: the liquid part is
76  * the total less the frozen part for a scheme with a total accumulator and
77  * can be slightly negative from round-off; an engine that bounds it should
78  * carry the negative part to the next step. Where a finer level covers the
79  * flood grid's level, ERF averages the finer accumulators down onto it
80  * after the flood's step (and, with super-droplets, at plotfiles and
81  * regrids); the next step carries that correction, of either sign, in the
82  * columns under the finer level. In single precision the per-step value is
83  * resolved to the round-off of the accumulator. nullptr unless the engine
84  * asks for it (FloodEngine::wants_surface_precip).
85  */
86  const amrex::MultiFab* precip_k0;
87 };
