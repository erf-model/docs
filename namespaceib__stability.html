Detailed Description

Bounds on the stability estimate of the immersed-forcing wall law. Without them a zero tangential wind one cell from the wall gives u* = 0, an Obukhov length of zero and zeta = z/0 (erf-model/ERF#4016).

Always on:

  • An Obukhov length derived from u* and the heat flux is held at |L| >= z_ref / 100, and every zeta = z / L at |zeta| <= 100, as on flat ground (ERF_MOSTStress.H). A prescribed erf.if_Olen is used as given: only its zeta is held, so one input means one L on every face and at every height.

Opt-in (WRF's revised surface layer, sf_sfclayrev):

  • erf.if_stability_wind_floor (default 0, WRF and the flat-ground WSMIN use 0.1 m/s) floors the wind behind the friction velocity of the stability estimate.
  • erf.if_psi_cap_factor (default 1, WRF uses 0.9) caps psi_m at factor ln(z / z0) in the velocity target and before it forms the friction velocity of the momentum law. The temperature forcing keeps caps of ln(z / z0): its surface-flux branch forms theta* from an Obukhov length that is not consistent with u*, which those caps keep from reaching the target in strongly unstable air.

amrex::Real  factor

noexcept

The largest psi (momentum or heat) the wall law uses at height z: factor ln(z / z0) for 0 < factor <= 1, and never more than ln(z / z0). For z > z0 a factor below 1 keeps ln(z / z0) - psi at no less than (1 - factor) ln(z / z0) > 0, so strongly unstable air still carries momentum and heat between the wall and the cell above; a cap of ln(z / z0) itself makes the target zero (momentum) or the value above (heat). For z <= z0, where the log law does not hold, the cap stays ln(z / z0).
