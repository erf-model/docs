Detailed Description

Where the simplified surface energy balance takes its sensible (H) and latent (LE) heat fluxes from.

The force-restore update removes H + LE from the ground. The air above receives the flux the lower surface layer applies, which it computes from the same skin temperature. Unless the two are the same flux the ground never loses what the air gains, so by default the balance takes the surface layer's flux.

Precedence, per field and per level:

  1. a land-surface model field of that name ("hfx" or "lh");
  2. the surface layer's applied flux, with seb_turbulent_flux_source = surface_layer and the flux field allocated: a zlo surface layer with any diffusion or closure, not on EB terrain; the latent flux only with a moisture model. An adiabatic surface layer has the field and a zero flux, so H (or LE) is then 0, and ERF warns at start-up if the scalar default it replaces is nonzero;
  3. the scalar default (seb_hfx_default or seb_lh_default).

The surface-layer flux is the conservative flux on the lowest z face (rho w'theta' and rho w'qv'), converted to W/m^2 by the same helpers as the sensible_heat_flux and latent_heat_flux 2D outputs, so the two cannot disagree. Positive is away from the surface, as in the balance.

Fill the SEB's H or LE [W/m^2] on the valid cells of seb_flux.

Only the valid cells are written in the land-surface and surface-layer cases (the default case sets the halo too): the force-restore update, the residual diagnostic and the seb_hfx/seb_lh outputs all read valid cells only, so the halo is never consumed. A consumer that needs it must fill it first.
