9 /**
10  * Cell sizes for formulas that use a lateral cell size as a length scale rather than to
11  * divide a difference: LES filter widths, scale-aware parameterizations, drag rates and
12  * per-area densities.
13  *
14  * On a domain with one cell in y and more than one in x (an x-z run), dy is arbitrary, so
15  * dx is used in its place; likewise dy replaces dx on a domain with one cell in x. The
16  * formula then keeps its 3-D form, as if the unresolved direction had the spacing of the
17  * resolved one. A single column (one cell in both) keeps the dx and dy of the inputs as
18  * its nominal column size.
19  *
20  * The level's own domain decides: a lateral direction with one cell is not refined
21  * (amr.ref_ratio_vect = r 1 r), so it has one cell on every level, and the spacing copied
22  * is that of the level.
23  *
24  * @param[in] geom geometry of the level
25  * @return dx, dy and dz with the substitution above
26  */
