Detailed Description

A refinement indicator from erf.refinement_indicators, tagged by ERF itself.

A cell is tagged when the conditions hold (all of them, or any of them, as require_all says) and the cell is inside the box, if there is one. A criterion without conditions tags every cell in its box.
