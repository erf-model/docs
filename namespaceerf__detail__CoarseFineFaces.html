Detailed Description

The mass flux a (rho u) / m of a horizontal momentum component, on the faces of its valid region and the ghost faces that the momentum, the area and the map factor all hold.

Parameters
mFace momenta, (rho u) or (rho v)
areaFace areas, ax or ay
mfMap factor on those faces, m_uy or m_vx

Fill the coarse/fine faces of one horizontal momentum component. Where cf_interp_mass_flux holds, the fill gives the mass flux, which is multiplied by the fine map factor and divided by the fine area on the faces it set; all other faces keep their values exactly.
