8 /**
9  * File names of the LSM data in a checkpoint.
10  *
11  * lsm_data[i] is written as "LsmData<i>". The Noah-MP snow and snowh fields
12  * were added in the middle of that numbering, which would shift every later
13  * index (the soil fields) by two and make an older checkpoint restart with the
14  * wrong fields. They are therefore stored under their own names and left out of
15  * the numbering, so every other field keeps the index it always had. A restart
16  * from a checkpoint that predates them keeps their initial values.
17  */
