Wind at a target height within one atmospheric column.

Brackets the target height by bisection on the column's terrain-following cell-center heights, averages the face velocities to cell centers at the two bracketing levels, and interpolates linearly between them. A target above the highest cell center takes that level's wind. A target below the lowest cell center takes the lowest center's wind as it is (below_first_log = false, which with a 20 m first cell hands the fire the 10 m wind as its 6.1 m wind, 12 % high at z0 = 0.1 m), or scaled down a neutral log profile of roughness z0_log (below_first_log = true), U(z) = U(z_1) ln(z/z0) / ln(z_1/z0), the better form for a first cell above the reference height. z_ground is the ground height the column heights are measured from; z_target - z_ground is the height above ground.

The wind at the lowest cell center of each atmospheric column, and its height above that column's ground.

For an engine that drives its fire with the k = 0 wind of the atmospheric columns rather than a wind interpolated to a height (LSFIRE does). The face velocities are averaged to the k = 0 cell center; the height is that center minus the column's ground, the mean of its four k = 0 nodes (the ground compute_fire_surface_height uses), or minus the domain floor without nodal heights.

Neutral log-law factor ln(z_to / z0) / ln(z_from / z0)

Every argument of the logarithms is kept valid whether or not the caller uses the result: clang evaluates a ternary's arm speculatively in Release, and with resampling off z_from is 0, so log(0) raised the divide-by-zero flag and tripped amrex.fpe_trap_zero on the first step. abs and the tiny offset are not selects, so nothing is folded back into the arm; for positive heights and roughness the values are unchanged. ln(z_to / z0) is floored at zero, so a roughness at or above z_to gives no wind rather than a reversed one, as the clamp below the first center does; the callers keep z0 well below both heights, where the floor is never reached.
