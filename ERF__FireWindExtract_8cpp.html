Wind at a target height within one atmospheric column.

Brackets the target height by bisection on the column's terrain-following cell-centre heights, averages the face velocities to cell centres at the two bracketing levels, and interpolates linearly between them. A target above the highest cell centre takes that level's wind. A target below the lowest cell centre takes the lowest centre's wind as it is (below_first_log = false, which with a 20 m first cell hands the fire the 10 m wind as its 6.1 m wind, 12 % high at z0 = 0.1 m), or scaled down a neutral log profile of roughness z0_log (below_first_log = true), U(z) = U(z_1) ln(z/z0) / ln(z_1/z0), the better form for a first cell above the reference height. z_ground is the ground height the column heights are measured from; z_target - z_ground is the height above ground.

The wind at the lowest cell centre of each atmospheric column, and its height above that column's ground.

For an engine that drives its fire with the k = 0 wind of the atmospheric columns rather than a wind interpolated to a height (LSFIRE does). The face velocities are averaged to the k = 0 cell centre; the height is that centre minus the column's ground, the mean of its four k = 0 nodes (the ground compute_fire_surface_height uses), or minus the domain floor without nodal heights.
