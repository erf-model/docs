◆ initialize()

virtual void DustModel::initialize ( const DustInit &  init)
pure virtual

Set the model up, and on a restart restore its state.

Reads the model's inputs (erf.dust.*). On a restart (init.restart_chkfile not empty) the model reads back what its write_checkpoint() wrote there. After this call, on a fresh start, ERF zeroes the dust scalars on every level.
