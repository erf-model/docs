Diagnose the state used by one microphysics cell.

In anelastic and pseudo-incompressible mode, which get their pressure from a projection rather than from the EOS, the conserved state supplies theta while the hydrostatic reference pressure supplied by BaseState supplies the thermodynamic pressure. In compressible mode, retain ERF's existing local-EOS diagnosis.
