Detailed Description

The one place that says how the two-stream force-restore balance reads the land mask (ERF::lmask_lev: 0 water, 1 land, 2 a building's column).

The balance is a model of soil. A column is left out of it when the mask is 0 there. The balance, its snow and hydrology steps, the albedo with snow, the average-down and the fill of a new level all ask through this file, so a change of the rule is one edit.
