Detailed Description

The wall cell of immersed-forcing terrain and the fraction-stress wall law (erf.if_wall_form = fraction_stress), shared by the momentum sources, the wall-face masks of the diffusion and the RANS wall distance so they cannot disagree.

A column of solid fractions beta (terrain_blanking) meets the air in its WALL CELL: a partial cell with fluid above it, or a fluid cell sitting on a solid cell. The surface is at h = z_bot + beta dz of that cell (beta = 0 for the fluid cell), the wall cell's fluid centroid at d1 = (1 - beta) dz / 2 above it and the centre of the cell above at d2 = (3/2 - beta) dz. The fraction-stress law takes the friction velocity u*^2 = (1 - beta) u*_own^2 + beta u*_ref^2, u*_own = kappa |u_t| / ln(d1/z0), u*_ref = kappa |u_t,ref| / ln(d2/z0), so a nearly full fluid cell uses its own velocity (on a face-aligned surface, beta = 0, that is the plain surface-layer law of flat ground at dz/2) and a thin fluid sliver the better resolved cell above, and applies it as a wall stress spread over the cell height, integrated exactly over the step: F = -rho C u_t, C = (1 - exp(-r dt)) / dt, r = u*^2 / (dz |u_t|). The resolved diffusive flux through the wall face (the bottom face of the wall cell) is switched off, so the column loses exactly u*^2 through the wall. This is form 7 of the kynema-sgf immersed terrain, written for the vertical columns of ERF's immersed forcing (horizontal velocity, vertical distances) and neutral stratification.
