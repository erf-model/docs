13 /**
14  * @file ERF_SEBLandAverageDown.H
15  * @brief The average-down of the two-stream force-restore balance's surface fields between
16  * two levels whose land masks hold water.
17  *
18  * The balance runs on land columns only (TwoStreamRadiation::advance), and a water column
19  * keeps the values it had at the start, at the restart, or when its level was built (the
20  * snow layer's water is set to zero there). An average over every fine column would then
21  * mix those held values into a coarse land column on a shore, and would overwrite a coarse
22  * water column that lies under fine land. Here a coarse land column takes the mean of the fine
23  * land columns under it; a coarse water column, and a coarse land column with no fine land
24  * column under it, keep their values.
25  *
26  * With no water column on either level this is not called: ERF then uses
27  * amrex::average_down, as before the mask.
28  *
29  * All fields go in one call: the fine land columns are counted once and one ParallelCopy
30  * carries every field. A pair with a null pointer on either side is left out.
31  *
32  * @param fine the 2D surface fields of the fine level (any number of components each)
33  * @param crse the same fields of the coarse level; updated under the fine level
34  * @param fine_mask the fine level's land mask (0 water), on the grids of @p fine
35  * @param crse_mask the coarse level's land mask (0 water), on the grids of @p crse
36  * @param ratio the refinement ratio, 1 in z
37  */
