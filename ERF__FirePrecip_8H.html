Detailed Description

Rain per column for the fuel moisture model from the microphysics surface precipitation accumulators, for an engine that reads the rain (FireEngine::wants_surface_precip).

Every Eulerian microphysics scheme with precipitation keeps a cumulative surface accumulator per atmospheric column (Kessler's rain_accum, SAM's and Morrison's rain, snow and graupel accumulations, ...), exposed through Microphysics::Get_Surface_Precip_Accumulation_Ptrs() with a factor that turns the scheme's native unit into kg/m2 of liquid water. One kg/m2 of liquid water is one millimetre of depth, so the change of the accumulation over a step, divided by the step, is the rain rate in mm/hr that the time-lag and stick moisture updates take. The fire layer keeps the accumulation it saw at the end of its previous step (checkpointed) and forms that difference every step; a fresh start begins at zero, which is where every scheme starts its accumulators.

Snow, graupel and hail count with their liquid-water equivalent: the fuel models have no frozen-precipitation branch. There is no canopy interception.
