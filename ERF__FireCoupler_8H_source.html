18 /**
19  * @file ERF_FireCoupler.H
20  * @brief The atmosphere side of the fire coupling, shared by every fire engine
21  *
22  * ERF holds one FireCoupler (std::unique_ptr<FireCoupler> m_fire_coupler),
23  * which owns the fire engine selected by erf.fire.engine. The coupler:
24  * - checks the ERF configuration a fire grid needs and builds that grid on
25  * the level it refines (erf.fire.anchor_level);
26  * - each step of that level, hands the engine the atmospheric state
27  * (FireForcing) and advances it; with lagged or synchronous coupling, or
28  * with the smoke tracer, it averages the fluxes the engine reports
29  * (FireFluxes) onto the atmospheric columns, where they wait for the next
30  * step (the one-step explicit lag of WRF-Fire);
31  * - at every Runge-Kutta stage of that next step, injects the lagged heat and
32  * vapour into the cell sources with the WRF-Fire exponential profile
33  * (ERF_FireAtmCoupling.H; lagged and synchronous coupling only) and the
34  * smoke tracer (ERF_FireSmokeEmission.H; erf.fire.smoke_enable);
35  * - holds the lagged fluxes for the boundary-layer scheme and the checkpoint.
36  *
37  * Coarser levels see the fire through average-down, finer levels not at all.
38  */
