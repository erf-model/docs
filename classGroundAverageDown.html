Detailed Description

Restriction of a quantity held in the ground layer of cells (a surface accumulation) from a fine level to the coarse level below it. Each coarse ground cell under a fine box that stands on the ground gets a scale times the sum of the rr[0]*rr[1] fine ground cells above it: the inverse area for a depth, 1 for an amount per cell. The coarse ground boxes, distributed with the fine boxes they lie under, are built once and serve every quantity restricted with them.
