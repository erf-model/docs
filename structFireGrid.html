Detailed Description

2D horizontal fire computational grid

Represents a 2D slab (kmax=1) with dimensions C*nx × C*ny × 1, where C is the refinement factor (erf.fire.grid_ratio) and (nx, ny) is the size of the region the atmospheric level's boxes cover: the whole domain on level 0, the refined region on a finer level. The members (ba, dm, geom, C, atm_lo, lev) are those of SurfaceGrid.
