Detailed Description

2D horizontal fire computational grid

Represents a 2D slab (kmax=1) with dimensions C*nx × C*ny × 1, where C is the refinement factor and (nx, ny) is the size of the region the atmospheric level's boxes cover: the whole domain on level 0, the refined region on a finer level.

◆ atm_lo

amrex::IntVect FireGrid::atm_lo {0, 0, 0}

Index, on the atmospheric level the grid refines, of the lower corner of the region the fire grid covers. The fire grid's own index space starts at zero at that corner, as every AMReX Geometry does, so fire cell (i, j) lies in atmospheric cell (i / C + atm_lo[0], j / C + atm_lo[1]). Zero on level 0.
