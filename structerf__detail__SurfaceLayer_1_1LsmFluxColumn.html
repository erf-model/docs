Detailed Description

The column whose LSM fluxes a face stress reads for cell (ii,jj), at most one cell beyond the grid. A column that another grid of the level or a periodic image holds is read where it is (in_domain only maps it into the domain or its periodic images). Any other column beyond the grid, at a coarse/fine boundary or outside a non-periodic domain, holds no current LSM flux, and compute_fluxes and compute_sfc_params_from_lsm_fluxes set its u* from the grid's nearest column, so it reads that column. Without a held mask, every column inside the domain counts as held.
