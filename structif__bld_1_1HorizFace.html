Detailed Description

A horizontal-momentum face (x or y) and its neighbours as the kernels read them. lo/hi are the neighbouring faces across the other horizontal direction: south/north for an x-face, west/east for a y-face. raw is the unthresholded blanking of the face (raw_face_blank); normal is true when, with the snap, the face joins a solid and a fluid cell (wall-normal).
