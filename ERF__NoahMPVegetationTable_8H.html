Detailed Description

Noah-MP's canopy-resistance parameters, roughness length and monthly leaf area index by land-use category, for the two-stream surface energy balance (erf.radiation.seb_vegetation_type).

The 20 categories of the modified IGBP MODIS land-use dataset and their values in &noahmp_modis_parameters of Submodules/Noah-MP/parameters/NoahmpTable.TBL – the table Noah-MP itself reads – copied here so the balance can use them in builds without Noah-MP. The CTest NoahMPSoilTable_MatchesSubmodule (Tests/check_noahmp_soil_table.py) fails if the two drift apart.

The day of the year, as Noah-MP counts it, of a date "YYYY-MM-DD[ HH:MM[:SS]]" (ERF's start_datetime).

The day is Noah-MP's DayJulianInYear, which PhenologyMainMod takes as 0 <= day < days_in_year. It is 0 at 00:00 on 1 January, and the time of day is added as a fraction. days_in_year is 365 or 366.

Returns
false when the date cannot be read, the month is outside 1-12, the day is outside the month, or the time is outside 00:00:00-23:59:60.
