Detailed Description

2D horizontal surface grid

A slab one cell thick with C*nx x C*ny cells, where (nx, ny) is the size of the region the atmospheric level's boxes cover: the whole domain on level 0, the refined region on a finer level.

◆ atm_lo

amrex::IntVect SurfaceGrid::atm_lo {0, 0, 0}

Index, on the atmospheric level the grid refines, of the lower corner of the region the grid covers. The grid's own index space starts at zero at that corner, as every AMReX Geometry does, so surface cell (i, j) lies in atmospheric cell (i / C + atm_lo[0], j / C + atm_lo[1]). Zero on level 0.
