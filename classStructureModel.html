Add the structures' force on the air to the momentum sources.

Face-centred sources on level lev [N/m^3, i.e. kg/(m^2 s^2)], added (not set), held fixed over the step. Called at every Runge-Kutta stage, after ERF's own sources. ERF applies no mask to them: the model keeps its kernels clear of embedded boundaries, immersed terrain and buildings.

With erf.coupling_type = TwoWay, ERF also calls it at the start of each step of every coarser level, with lev = level() and zeroed MultiFabs on the grids of level() as of the last advance() (they may have ghost faces, which the model leaves alone). It adds the source of its last advance(), as at a stage. ERF restricts that source onto the coarser level, keeping its integral, and holds it over that level's step: the air under the finer level then feels the force while the coarser level steps, and the coarse/fine faces, which the finer level takes from the coarser one, get the force once. Before the first advance() and after a regrid of level(), until the next advance(), ERF does not call it for the coarser levels.

◆ level()

virtual int StructureModel::level ( int  finest_level) const
pure virtual

The level the model couples to.

ERF calls advance() on this level only, and add_momentum_sources() for this level only (see add_momentum_sources() for the coarser levels). Aborts, naming the model's input, when the model cannot couple to a level of this run (for example a level above finest_level).

The level must be the finest level that covers the model's support. On a refined level the model's points and their kernels must lie inside the level's boxes, also after a regrid (erf_actuator::points_covered_by checks it). The force reaches the coarser levels through ERF's averaging down and, with TwoWay coupling, as a source restricted from this level. It never reaches a finer level: averaging down would overwrite the forced cells with that level's unforced data, so ERF stops the run when a finer level's boxes overlap support().

Fields the ERF plotfile can carry, by name.

The names the deck may list in erf.plot_vars_*; they start with the model's own prefix (for example "conductor_") so they never collide with an ERF variable. ERF stops the run on an empty name, a name listed twice, or a name ERF or another model already uses. The answer depends on the model's inputs only. The default lists none.

◆ support()

virtual std::vector<amrex::RealBox> StructureModel::support ( ) const
pure virtual

Where the model's force can be nonzero this step.

Boxes in physical coordinates [m] (z as the height of z_phys_nd on a terrain-following mesh) that hold every point together with its kernel's reach, as of the last advance(). The same on every rank. ERF checks them against the next finer level's boxes after each advance() (see level()). The boxes may extend past the domain; a periodic direction wraps. Every box needs its low end at or below its high end in each direction and no NaN (an infinite end is allowed); ERF stops the run otherwise, naming the model.
