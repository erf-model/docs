17 /**
18  * @file ERF_DustModel.H
19  * @brief The interface between ERF and a dust model
20  *
21  * A dust model emits dust from the ground into the dust scalars of the
22  * conserved state, settles and deposits it, and writes its own output. ERF
23  * owns the dust scalars: ERF_NUM_DUST_SCALARS (CMake; NUM_DUST_SCALARS in GNU
24  * make) of them, starting at RhoDust_comp (ERF_IndexDefines.H), advected and
25  * diffused like every passive scalar and zero at the start of a run. A model
26  * adds its tendencies to them in add_sources() and keeps everything else (its
27  * surface grid, its fields, its particles) itself.
28  *
29  * ERF itself contains no model. A model lives in its own directory outside ERF
30  * (DUST_HOME in GNU make, ERF_DUST_MODEL_DIRS in CMake) and registers itself
31  * with ERF_REGISTER_DUST_MODEL (ERF_DustModelRegistry.H). erf.dust.enable =
32  * true turns dust on; erf.dust.model names the model, and may be left out
33  * when one model is compiled in.
34  *
35  * ERF calls, in this order:
36  * - initialize() once, at the end of start-up (after the fire coupling's
37  * initialization), which on a restart also restores the model's state;
38  * - add_sources() at every Runge-Kutta stage of every level, right after
39  * ERF's own sources are built and before the fire coupling adds its fluxes;
40  * - advance() once per step of level 0, after the step;
41  * - write_output() at start-up (step 0, before the first plotfile), after
42  * each step and at the end of the run, each time before ERF writes a
43  * checkpoint, so a checkpoint records the model's output of that step as
44  * written;
45  * - write_checkpoint() each time ERF writes a checkpoint.
46  *
47  * Memory contract, as for fire engines (ERF_FireEngine.H): every MultiFab in
48  * DustInit, DustStep and the add_sources() arguments is an ordinary AMReX
49  * MultiFab in the default arena (device memory on a GPU build) and a
50  * temporary of the call; a model keeps no pointer to it.
51  */
