Compute the horizontal components of the pressure gradient, carrying the hydrostatic part of the perturbational pressure to the height of the face along dp'/dz = -g rho' and only the non-hydrostatic remainder along a difference of p'.

At an x-face (i,j,k) the two cell centers that straddle the face sit at different physical heights, so each must be carried a signed distance 

dz_int = 1/2 ( z_cc(i,j,k) - z_cc(i-1,j,k) )

to reach the height of the face. ChainRule does this with a centered difference of p'. Over steep terrain in nearly hydrostatic flow that difference is dominated by its hydrostatic part, dp'/dz ~ -g rho', which is large compared with the horizontal gradient being sought, so its truncation error dominates the answer. Here we split 

dp'/dz = -g rho' + r,    r = dp'/dz + g rho',

integrate the first term exactly over the segment and difference only r, so that 

gpx = [p'(i,j,k) - p'(i-1,j,k)]/dx + met_h_xi * 1/2 * [(g rhot - r)(i,j,k) + (g rhot - r)(i-1,j,k)]

where met_h_xi = 2*dz_int/dx, rhot is rho' reconstructed linearly and evaluated at the midpoint of each of the two extrapolation segments, and r is formed in each column by nonhydro_residual_cc. Two properties follow:

  • On every w-face r is exactly gpz + buoyancy as the w-equation forms them (with buoyancy_type 1), so in a state that the w-equation sees as balanced r vanishes and gpx is the pure hydrostatic reconstruction: it vanishes identically whenever rho' is linear in z and p' is its hydrostatic integral, whatever the terrain slope.
  • Substituting the definition of r shows gpx equals the ChainRule stencil plus g * met_h_xi * (rhot - rhohat), with rhohat the Delta z-weighted average of rho' on the two adjacent w-faces. That correction vanishes as the mesh is refined, so unlike a purely hydrostatic reconstruction this remains consistent in strongly non-hydrostatic flow.

Note that the stencil needs no branch on the sign of dz_int, since the formula is symmetric under dz_int -> -dz_int, and that it reaches only to k-1 and k+1.

Non-hydrostatic residual of the vertical pressure gradient, dp'/dz + g rho', brought to the cell center. On each w-face this residual is exactly gpz + buoyancy as the dycore forms them (with buoyancy_rhopert), so it vanishes wherever the w-equation sees no net vertical force. In the interior the two face values are combined with the same Delta z weighting that the centered difference of ChainRule implies; at the bottom and top the single interior face is used.
