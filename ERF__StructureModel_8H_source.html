15 /**
16  * @file ERF_StructureModel.H
17  * @brief The interface between ERF and a model of structures in the flow
18  *
19  * A structure model represents bodies that the air loads and that push back on
20  * the air: power-line conductors and their towers, for example. It samples the
21  * wind at its own points, advances its own dynamics, and returns a momentum
22  * source that ERF adds to the air at every Runge-Kutta stage. The velocity
23  * sampler and the force spreading of the actuator core (Source/MovingBodies/Core,
24  * ERF_ActuatorSampling.H and ERF_ActuatorSpreading.H) are the tools a model is
25  * expected to use for both.
26  *
27  * ERF itself contains no structure model. A model lives in its own directory
28  * outside ERF (STRUCTURE_HOME in GNU make, ERF_STRUCTURE_MODEL_DIRS in CMake)
29  * and registers itself with ERF_REGISTER_STRUCTURE_MODEL
30  * (ERF_StructureModelRegistry.H); erf.structures.model selects it by name.
31  * Tests/StructureModelExample is a complete model directory.
32  *
33  * ERF calls, in this order:
34  * - the registered creator once, in the ERF constructor (the model reads its
35  * own inputs there, under its own prefix);
36  * - level() each time ERF needs the level the model couples to;
37  * - initialize() once, after the initial data (or the checkpoint) exist and
38  * before the step-0 plotfile; on a restart the model restores its own state
39  * there, from StructureInit::restart_chkfile. It is not called again after a
40  * regrid: what the model needs of the mesh it reads from StructureStep each step;
41  * - advance() once per step of level(), with the state at the start of the step,
42  * before that step's Runge-Kutta stages;
43  * - support() right after each advance(): when a finer level than level() exists,
44  * ERF stops the run if that level's boxes overlap the support (the force would
45  * be lost there, see level());
46  * - add_momentum_sources() at every Runge-Kutta stage of level(), and, with
47  * erf.coupling_type = TwoWay, once at the start of each step of every coarser
48  * level, into zeroed MultiFabs on the grids of level(), which ERF restricts
49  * onto that level (see add_momentum_sources());
50  * - write_checkpoint() each time ERF writes a checkpoint;
51  * - finalize() once, when the run ends normally, after the last plotfile and checkpoint;
52  * - plot_var_names() each time ERF builds its plotfile variable list, and
53  * fill_plot_var() for each listed name on every level of each plotfile.
54  *
55  * Memory contract. Every MultiFab ERF hands a model is an ordinary AMReX
56  * MultiFab in the default arena (device memory on a GPU build) and is valid
57  * only during the call. A model keeps no pointer to it after the call returns.
58  */

102 /**
103  * @brief What a structure model may read from the fire, when the run has one
104  *
105  * The fire engine's fields (the getters of ERF_FireEngine.H) on the fire grid: a
106  * horizontal grid, one cell in z, that refines one atmospheric level
107  * (erf.fire.anchor_level). That level need not be the one the model couples to.
108  * Fire cell (i, j) has its centre at geom.ProbLo() + ((i + 1/2) dx, (j + 1/2) dy),
109  * in the coordinates of the model's own points.
110  *
111  * The fields are the engine's state at time: the time ERF assigns to the fire's
112  * level at the end of the fire's last step, or the time ERF started or restarted
113  * from before that step. The fire steps after the dycore of its own level. A
114  * model on the fire's level or a coarser one therefore sees the fire at its own
115  * step's time, exactly; a model
116  * on a finer level sees it up to one step of the fire's level ahead of its own
117  * step. On a restart, until the fire's first step, they are what the engine
118  * restored from the checkpoint (an engine that does not restore a field holds
119  * its initial value there). Each field is spread over the ranks as the fire grid is: a model that
120  * needs the fire at its own points reduces over the ranks itself (every rank
121  * calls advance()). Like the flow fields, they are temporaries of the call.
122  */
123 struct StructureFire
124 {
125  const amrex::Geometry& geom; ///< Geometry of the fire grid [m] (one cell in z)
126  int lev; ///< The atmospheric level the fire grid refines
127  double time; ///< The time the fields belong to [s] (see above)
128  const amrex::MultiFab* heat_release; ///< Heat released [W/m2] on the fire grid (FireEngine::heat_release); nullptr if the engine has none
129  const amrex::MultiFab* level_set; ///< Level set on the fire grid, negative where burned (FireEngine::level_set); nullptr if the engine has none
130  /// Area burned so far [m2] (FireEngine::burned_area), negative when the engine does not

170  /**
171  * @brief The level the model couples to
172  *
173  * ERF calls advance() on this level only, and add_momentum_sources() for
174  * this level only (see add_momentum_sources() for the coarser levels).
175  * Aborts, naming the model's input, when the model cannot couple to a level
176  * of this run (for example a level above finest_level).
177  *
178  * The level must be the finest level that covers the model's support. On a
179  * refined level the model's points and their kernels must lie inside the
180  * level's boxes, also after a regrid (erf_actuator::points_covered_by checks
181  * it). The force reaches the coarser levels through ERF's averaging down and,
182  * with TwoWay coupling, as a source restricted from this level.
183  * It never reaches a finer level: averaging down would overwrite the forced
184  * cells with that level's unforced data, so ERF stops the run when a finer
185  * level's boxes overlap support().
186  *
187  * @param[in] finest_level ERF's finest level now
188  */

196  * reach, as of the last advance(). The same on every rank. ERF checks them
197  * against the next finer level's boxes after each advance() (see level()).
198  * The boxes may extend past the domain; a periodic direction wraps.

225  /**
226  * @brief Add the structures' force on the air to the momentum sources
227  *
228  * Face-centred sources on level lev [N/m^3, i.e. kg/(m^2 s^2)], added (not set),
229  * held fixed over the step. Called at every Runge-Kutta stage, after ERF's
230  * own sources. ERF applies no mask to them: the model keeps its kernels clear
231  * of embedded boundaries, immersed terrain and buildings.
232  *
233  * With erf.coupling_type = TwoWay, ERF also calls it at the start of each step
234  * of every coarser level, with lev = level() and zeroed MultiFabs on the grids
235  * of level() as of the last advance() (they may have ghost faces, which the
236  * model leaves alone). It adds the source of its last advance(), as at a
237  * stage. ERF restricts that source onto the coarser level, keeping the force
238  * it puts on the air (its integral over the faces' control volumes, with the
239  * mesh Jacobian on a stretched or terrain-following mesh), and holds it over
240  * that level's step: the air under the finer level then feels the force while
241  * the coarser level steps, and the coarse/fine faces, which the finer level
242  * takes from the coarser one, get the force once.
243  * Before the first advance() and after a regrid of level(), until the next
244  * advance(), ERF does not call it for the coarser levels. ERF writes the
245  * source to its checkpoint, so after a restart, until the first advance(), it
