19 /**
20  * Perturbation of rho and rho*qv for a temperature change dT and a vapour change dqv.
21  *
22  * With perturb_T, the new density gives T = T_old + dT at the pressure of the new state,
23  * P(rho*theta, qv + dqv); with dqv = 0 that is the old pressure. Without perturb_T the

33  * @param[in] rdOcp R_d / c_p
34  * @param[out] drho perturbation of rho
