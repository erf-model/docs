Detailed Description

The fluxes a fire engine hands to the atmosphere after its step.

Both fields live on the fire grid (FireGrid::ba, FireGrid::dm), per unit ground area, and are positive into the atmosphere. Each is the time mean over exactly the interval of the last advance(), [forcing.time, forcing.time + forcing.dt], whatever steps the engine takes inside it: an engine that subcycles accumulates its fluxes over its substeps and divides by forcing.dt. The coupling averages them onto the atmospheric columns and injects them, unchanged, at every stage of the next ERF step of the fire grid's level (the one-step lag of WRF-Fire; erf.fire.coupling_type = synchronous only changes the wind the engine saw).

The latent flux is the energy of the water vapour released, so the vapour mass flux is latent / L_v [kg/m2/s] with L_v of ERF_Constants.H; an engine that computes a mass flux multiplies by that same L_v. The pointers stay valid until the engine's next advance(). Sensible and latent pointers are independently optional: nullptr means no flux for that channel on this step, without suppressing a valid flux from the other channel.
