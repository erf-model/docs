Detailed Description

Fire grid data structure and construction.

Defines a 2D grid for fire simulation on the horizontal plane, refined by factor C relative to one atmospheric AMR level (erf.fire.anchor_level, the finest level by default). The fire grid covers that level's boxes, keeps their x-y decomposition and their MPI owners, and its index space starts at zero at the lower corner of the region they cover. It is the surface grid of the surface-coupling layer (ERF_SurfaceGrid.H) under the fire coupling's name; the functions below call that layer with the fire coupling's names.

Exchange the ghost cells of a fire-grid MultiFab and fill the ones outside a non-periodic face of the domain by copying the nearest interior value (surface_fill_boundary).

FillBoundary alone leaves those cells untouched, so with an inflow-outflow atmosphere (the fire grid inherits its periodicity) the gradient stencil of the level set read whatever the allocator had left there. Whether that was benign depended on the box layout: one box per rank happened to run, two boxes on a rank blew the level set up at the inflow face within three steps. Every fire-grid exchange goes through here.
