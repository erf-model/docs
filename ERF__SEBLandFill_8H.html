Fill the halo of a coarse 2D surface field before it is interpolated.

FillBoundary sets the interior and periodic ghost columns. Across a non-periodic face a ghost column takes the nearest column inside the domain: the slope stencil of the interpolater reads it for every boundary type. A periodic ghost column is left as FillBoundary set it. InterpFromCoarseLevel reads it twice, from this halo and from the periodic image of the valid column, and the two must agree.

Fill fine from crse when the coarse land mask holds water.

A water column holds a value the balance never updated. The fill keeps it out of the land columns:

  • A fine column whose parent and the parent's eight neighbours are all land takes the conservative linear interpolation, as on a level with no water.
  • Elsewhere no slope is used: the slope of a coarse column next to water is formed with the held value. A fine land column over a coarse land column takes the parent's value. A fine land column over a coarse water column takes the mean of the land columns among the parent's eight neighbours.
  • A fine land column whose parent and all eight neighbours are water has no land source. It takes the parent's held value and is counted.
  • A fine water column in that region takes its parent's value.

Neighbours beyond a non-periodic domain face are left out. RemakeLevel copies the old fine values on top afterwards, so only columns new to the level keep this fill.
