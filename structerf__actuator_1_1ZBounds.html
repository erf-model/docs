Detailed Description

The lowest and highest ground and the smallest and largest spacing of the levels (m) of a mesh, and, over one rank's boxes (mesh_z_bounds with over_all_ranks false) or the level's (mesh_z_levels), a table of each node level's lowest and highest height and of the cells those boxes hold, which bounds the cells a height may lie in whether or not the boxes reach the ground (a column split in z, a level aloft).

The cells k that heights from zl to zh may lie in, whatever the column: [kb, kt], clamped to [klo - 1, khi + 1] (so a range outside the domain stays outside it); [klo, khi] without bounds. With the table: exactly the boxes' cells k whose lowest bottom node is not above zh and whose highest top node is not below zl, or kb > kt when there is none. zl or zh may be infinite (not NaN): heights from -infinity take every cell up to zh's, and a range at one infinity takes no cell of the domain.
