14 /**
15  * Wake sampling lines behind one rotor: at each downstream distance x/D along the horizontal
16  * projection of the rotor axis through the hub (a wake follows the wind at hub height, not a
17  * tilted shaft), one lateral line (horizontal, normal to that direction) and one vertical
18  * line, each of num_points points spanning +-half_width diameters about the centre. The
19  * sampled velocities are written as they are and accumulated into a running time average.
20  *
21  * Point order: for each x/D, the lateral line then the vertical line, each from -half_width
22  * to +half_width; the vertical line is clipped at z_min (the ground), so its lowest point may
23  * sit above -half_width. Every point's offset s (in diameters) is recorded in the files. The
24  * lateral line runs along (-axis_y, axis_x, 0), from the right of the downstream direction to
25  * its left; the vertical line runs up. Positions in m, ERF domain coordinates; velocities in m/s.
26  */
