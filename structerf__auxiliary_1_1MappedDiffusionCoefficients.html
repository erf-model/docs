Detailed Description

Face coefficients under the host's rule: an eddy coefficient is already density weighted, and a kinematic molecular coefficient is multiplied by the face-averaged density. Horizontal faces read horizontal.eddy_h_comp and vertical faces vertical.eddy_v_comp.
