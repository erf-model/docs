Factor by which to scale erf.fixed_dt (and erf.fixed_fast_dt) on a level so that the fixed step respects the CFL limit (erf-model/ERF#5238). The fixed step exceeds the limit when its Courant number fixed_dt * cfl_rate is above FixedDtCFLLimitValue(erf.cfl) = min(erf.cfl, 0.8), and is then scaled to exactly that Courant number, so the scaled step is continuous in the Courant number. Returns 1 when the fixed step is within the limit, or when there is no fixed step, no CFL limit (cfl <= 0) or no constraining rate on the level.
