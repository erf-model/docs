Detailed Description

Physical heights at the face centres of a cell, from the nodal heights z_nd.

On a mesh other than ConstantDz, geometry's prob_lo[2], prob_hi[2] and CellSize(2) only bound and step the computational coordinate, so problem setups take their heights from z_nd (and z_cc) there.
