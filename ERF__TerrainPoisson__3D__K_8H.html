A masked face is a Neumann face of the solve region: a face of the region inside the domain (a coarse/fine face of a refined level) or a Neumann (wall, inflow) face of the domain. The even mirror that TerrainPoisson::apply_bcs puts in the ghost cell zeroes the normal-gradient part of the flux through such a face but not the terrain cross term, so on sloping terrain the face would let correction through; with the face masked the whole flux through it is zero, as in AMReX's MLTerrainPoisson. Faces outside the mask are untouched, bit for bit.
