Detailed Description

Initial temperature and vapour perturbations of a moist column, applied together.

Shared by the BOMEX, GATE and RICO setups (and DYCOMS, which uses the RICO one). rho*theta is held, so the temperature perturbation is stored as a density change; the vapour mixing ratio after the perturbation is qv + dqv, whatever the density.

Perturbation of rho and rho*qv for a temperature change dT and a vapour change dqv.

With perturb_T, the new density gives T = T_old + dT at the pressure of the new state, P(rho*theta, qv + dqv); with dqv = 0 that is the old pressure. Without perturb_T the density is left as it is. rho*qv then holds qv + dqv on the new density.
