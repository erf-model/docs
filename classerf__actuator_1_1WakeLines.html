Detailed Description

Wake sampling lines behind one rotor: at each downstream distance x/D along the horizontal projection of the rotor axis through the hub (a wake follows the wind at hub height, not a tilted shaft), one lateral line (horizontal, normal to that direction) and one vertical line, each of num_points points spanning +-half_width diameters about the centre. The sampled velocities are written as they are and accumulated into a running time average.

Point order: for each x/D, the lateral line then the vertical line, each from -half_width to +half_width; the vertical line is clipped at z_min (the ground), so its lowest point may sit above -half_width. Every point's offset s (in diameters) is recorded in the files. The lateral line runs along (-axis_y, axis_x, 0), from the right of the downstream direction to its left; the vertical line runs up. Positions in m, ERF domain coordinates; velocities in m/s.
