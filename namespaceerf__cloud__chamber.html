enum erf_cloud_chamber::WallFace : unsigned char

Stage 1 wall metadata. This is deliberately a small, trivially copyable description: it can be captured by an AMReX device kernel without virtual dispatch. PrescribedState is the only active treatment. A future wall factory can consume the face identity and return momentum, thermal-scalar, and vapor fluxes without changing the chamber initializer.
