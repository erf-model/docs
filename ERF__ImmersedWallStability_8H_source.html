10 /**
11  * Bounds on the stability estimate of the immersed-forcing wall law. Without them a zero
12  * tangential wind one cell from the wall gives u* = 0, an Obukhov length of zero and zeta = z/0
13  * (erf-model/ERF#4016).
14  *
15  * Always on:
16  * - An Obukhov length derived from u* and the heat flux is held at |L| >= z_ref / 100, and every
17  * zeta = z / L at |zeta| <= 100, as on flat ground (ERF_MOSTStress.H). A prescribed
18  * erf.if_Olen is used as given: only its zeta is held, so one input means one L on every face
19  * and at every height.
20  *
21  * Opt-in (WRF's revised surface layer, sf_sfclayrev):
22  * - erf.if_stability_wind_floor (default 0, WRF and the flat-ground WSMIN use 0.1 m/s) floors the
23  * wind behind the friction velocity of the stability estimate.
24  * - erf.if_psi_cap_factor (default 1, WRF uses 0.9) caps psi_m at factor ln(z / z0) in the
25  * velocity target and before it forms the friction velocity of the momentum law. The
26  * temperature forcing keeps caps of ln(z / z0): its surface-flux branch forms theta* from an
27  * Obukhov length that is not consistent with u*, which those caps keep from reaching the target
28  * in strongly unstable air.
29  */

43  /**
44  * An Obukhov length derived from u* and the heat flux, with |L| no smaller than
45  * z_ref / zeta_max(), so that z / L lies within +-zeta_max() for every z <= z_ref and the
46  * zero L of a zero u* is never divided by. The sign is kept: L = -0 is the unstable bound and
47  * L = +0 the stable one. Unchanged, bit for bit, when |L| >= z_ref / zeta_max(). Not for a
48  * prescribed L, whose bound would then depend on the local cell size; see bounded_zeta.
49  */

52  {
53  return std::copysign(amrex::max(std::abs(Olen), z_ref / zeta_max()), Olen);

68  /**
69  * The largest psi (momentum or heat) the wall law uses at height z: factor ln(z / z0) for
70  * 0 < factor <= 1, and never more than ln(z / z0). For z > z0 a factor below 1 keeps
71  * ln(z / z0) - psi at no less than (1 - factor) ln(z / z0) > 0, so strongly unstable air
72  * still carries momentum and heat between the wall and the cell above; a cap of ln(z / z0)
73  * itself makes the target zero (momentum) or the value above (heat). For z <= z0, where the
74  * log law does not hold, the cap stays ln(z / z0).
75  */
76  AMREX_GPU_HOST_DEVICE AMREX_FORCE_INLINE
77  amrex::Real psi_cap (amrex::Real z, amrex::Real z0, amrex::Real factor) noexcept
78  {
79  const amrex::Real ln = std::log(z / z0);
