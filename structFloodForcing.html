Detailed Description

The atmospheric state a flood engine reads for one step.

Every field lives on the level the flood grid refines (erf.flood.anchor_level), in that level's index space; the engine maps them onto its own grid (FloodGrid::atm_lo gives the corner of the flood region, fill_flood_from_atm_k0 copies a column field onto the flood grid). The k = 0 slabs hold one cell in z per box of the level. The slabs are temporaries of FloodCoupler::advance(): an engine reads them during its advance() and keeps no pointer to them.

◆ precip_k0

const amrex::MultiFab* FloodForcing::precip_k0

Surface precipitation of this step on the k = 0 slabs [kg/m2, equal to mm of liquid water]: component 0 the liquid part, component 1 the frozen part (snow, graupel and hail as liquid-water equivalent; Morrison and WSM6 count their cloud-water fallout, and Morrison its cloud-ice fallout, as liquid). It is the change of the microphysics accumulators over the step, so the sum over the steps of a run is the change of ERF's own accumulation over the run. Not bounded at zero: the liquid part is the total less the frozen part for a scheme with a total accumulator and can be slightly negative from round-off; an engine that bounds it should carry the negative part to the next step. Where a finer level covers the flood grid's level, ERF averages the finer accumulators down onto it after the flood's step (and, with super-droplets, at plotfiles and regrids); the next step carries that correction, of either sign, in the columns under the finer level. In single precision the per-step value is resolved to the round-off of the accumulator. nullptr unless the engine asks for it (FloodEngine::wants_surface_precip).
