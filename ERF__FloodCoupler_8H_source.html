17 /**
18  * @file ERF_FloodCoupler.H
19  * @brief The atmosphere side of the flood coupling, shared by every flood engine
20  *
21  * ERF holds one FloodCoupler (std::unique_ptr<FloodCoupler> m_flood_coupler),
22  * which owns the flood engine selected by erf.flood.engine. The coupler:
23  * - checks the ERF configuration a flood grid needs and builds that grid on
24  * the level it refines (erf.flood.anchor_level);
25  * - keeps the microphysics surface precipitation accumulation it saw last, and
26  * each step of that level hands the engine the change since then (liquid and
27  * frozen), with the wind after the dycore (FloodForcing);
28  * - writes the engine's fields into the ERF plotfile on request;
29  * - records the flood grid in the checkpoint and refuses a restart onto
30  * another one.
31  *
32  * The coupling is one-way: nothing returns to the atmosphere.
33  */
