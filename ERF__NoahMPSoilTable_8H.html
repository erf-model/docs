Detailed Description

Noah-MP's soil parameters by soil category, for the two-stream surface energy balance (erf.radiation.seb_soil_type).

The 19 categories and the values of the STAS soil dataset (&noahmp_soil_stas_parameters) in Submodules/Noah-MP/parameters/NoahmpTable.TBL – the table Noah-MP itself reads – copied here so the balance can use them in builds without Noah-MP. The CTest NoahMPSoilTable_MatchesSubmodule (Tests/check_noahmp_soil_table.py) fails if the two drift apart.
