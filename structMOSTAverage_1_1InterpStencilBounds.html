Detailed Description

Lateral bounds on the interpolation stencil of the MOST reference points in one box.

The average of field n (U, V, W, T, Qv) reads the positions of the columns in reach[n], its own staggered box grown by the ghost cells the averages fill and the radius, and interpolates from the two cells in x and in y that bracket each point; those cells must lie in held[n], the cells its FAB holds. The tangential speed and virtual temperature averages read U, V, T and Qv at cell-centered columns, which lie in the reaches of those fields.

get_ng_fill leaves the stencil one cell of room at the outermost columns, so a point displaced along the terrain normal by more than a cell can leave the data there, whatever the number of ghost cells.

As set_norm_indices_T does without interpolation, a displacement past the ghost cells then uses the nearest point held; only ghost columns near the edge of the box are moved, whose averages FillBoundary overwrites where boxes abut.
