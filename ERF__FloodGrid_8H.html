Detailed Description

Flood grid data structure, construction and the maps between the flood grid and the atmospheric columns.

A 2D grid on the horizontal plane, refined by grid_ratio relative to one atmospheric AMR level (erf.flood.anchor_level, the finest level by default). The flood grid covers that level's boxes, keeps their x-y decomposition and their MPI owners, and its index space starts at zero at the lower corner of the region they cover. It is the surface grid of the surface-coupling layer (ERF_SurfaceGrid.H) under the flood coupling's name, as the fire grid is; the functions below call that layer with the flood coupling's names.
