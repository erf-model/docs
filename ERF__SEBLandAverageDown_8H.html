Detailed Description

The average-down of the two-stream force-restore balance's surface fields between two levels whose land masks hold water.

The balance runs on land columns only (TwoStreamRadiation::advance), and a water column keeps the values it had at the start, at the restart, or when its level was built (the snow layer's water is set to zero there). An average over every fine column would then mix those held values into a coarse land column on a shore, and would overwrite a coarse water column that lies under fine land. Here a coarse land column takes the mean of the fine land columns under it; a coarse water column, and a coarse land column with no fine land column under it, keep their values.

With no water column on either level this is not called: ERF then uses amrex::average_down, as before the mask.

All fields go in one call: the fine land columns are counted once and one ParallelCopy carries every field. A pair with a null pointer on either side is left out.

Parameters
finethe 2D surface fields of the fine level (any number of components each)
crsethe same fields of the coarse level; updated under the fine level
fine_maskthe fine level's land mask (0 water), on the grids of fine
crse_maskthe coarse level's land mask (0 water), on the grids of crse
ratiothe refinement ratio, 1 in z
