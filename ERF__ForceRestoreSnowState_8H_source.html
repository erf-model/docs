18  * @brief The per-level state of the force-restore snow layer (ERF_ForceRestoreSnow.H,
19  * erf.radiation.seb_snow_enable): the snow water equivalent and the running totals
20  * that close each column's snow budget.
21  *
22  * Owned by TwoStreamRadiation, beside the force-restore hydrology
23  * (ERF_ForceRestoreHydrologyState.H) and the precipitation rate of the step
24  * (ERF_ForceRestorePrecipRate.H). step(...) after the balance, and before the hydrology
25  * step, applies one snow step to every open column: it takes the frozen precipitation,
