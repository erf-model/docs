13 /**
14  * The hydrometeors a microphysics scheme moved across each z-face by sedimentation during
15  * its most recent Advance on a level.
16  *
17  * down(i,j,k,n) is the amount of the conserved state component cons_comp[n] (rho*q, so
18  * kg m^-3 for a mixing ratio and m^-3 for a number concentration) that crossed z-face
19  * (i,j,k) downward during that Advance, per unit horizontal area: kg m^-2 or m^-2. Face k
20  * is the bottom face of cell k, so the face at the domain bottom holds what reached the
21  * ground. down is defined on the level's grids, z-nodal, and is zeroed at the start of
22  * every Advance.
23  *
24  * A scheme records the fluxes its cell updates actually used, so that (up to the clipping
25  * of negative values) the change of rho*q*dz in a cell is down on its top face minus down on
26  * its bottom face. Schemes whose sedimentation has no face fluxes of its own (the
27  * semi-Lagrangian WSM6/WDM6 solvers) reconstruct them with sedimentation_add_column_sums below.
28  */
29 /**
30  * The inflow a fine patch receives through its top face, handed to a scheme for one Advance
31  * (NullMoist::Set_Sed_Inflow). It is z-nodal on the level's grids, with the components of
32  * the level's SedimentationFlux plus a flag, comp inflow_flag_comp(): where the flag is 1,
33  * face (i,j,k) is the top of a fine column that stops short of the domain top, and
34  * component n holds the amount of cons_comp[n] entering through it during the Advance, per
35  * unit horizontal area (as SedimentationFlux::down). Elsewhere everything is zero.
36  */

64 /**
65  * Add sign * (sum over m = k..khi of w(m)) to sed(i,j,k,n) for every k in [klo, khi].
66  *
67  * Called with sign = +1 and w(m) = rho*q*dz before a sedimentation step and with sign = -1
68  * and the same w after it, this adds to each face k the mass that crossed it downward: what
69  * the cells above it lost. The face above khi gets nothing (no inflow).
70  */
