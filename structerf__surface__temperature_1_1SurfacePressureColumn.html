Detailed Description

The column whose lowest state cell gives the surface pressure of a surface cell, including the lateral halo of a planar surface field.

The choice depends only on the domain, never on the box that holds the cell: where a box seam meets a non-periodic domain edge, two boxes hold the same halo cell, and a column taken from each box's own valid region gave the two copies different values, which a restart cannot reproduce. In a non-periodic direction the index is clamped to the domain; in a periodic one it is kept, so that the state's periodic ghost cell is read. Either way the cell read lies in the domain or its periodic image, so it is a valid cell of this box or a ghost cell that the state's FillBoundary has filled.
