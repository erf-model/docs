4 /**
5  * \file ERF_WRFSurfaceFields.H
6  *
7  * Land and soil fields of a wrfinput file that no land model takes: leaf area, vegetation
8  * fraction, deep soil temperature, roughness, albedo, background albedo, sea ice, snow,
9  * and the soil temperature and moisture profiles with the depths of their layers.
10  *
11  * ERF::init_from_wrfinput fills this holder when the two-stream force-restore balance
12  * (erf.radiation.seb_prognostic_enable) runs without a land model. Every field is
13  * optional: a file without it leaves get() null. The holder keeps the values as the file
14  * gives them, in the file's units; it applies no physics.
15  *
16  * A field that holds a value outside its catalog range, or one that is not finite, or
17  * that the level's files do not cover, is reported and not held: get() is null, as for a
18  * field the file does not have. The code that takes a field decides whether its absence
19  * stops the run.
20  *
21  * The data are those of the initial time. They are written to the checkpoint and read
22  * back on a restart. A regrid carries them onto the new grids: a column the old grids
23  * of the level held keeps its values, and any other column takes those of the column of
24  * the coarser level it lies in (piecewise constant, so a flag stays a flag, a soil
25  * profile stays the profile of one column, and no value leaves the catalog range). A
26  * level without a file of its own (made by tagging, at the start or by a regrid) takes
27  * every field of the coarser level in the same way.
28  *
29  * The class has no NetCDF dependency: fill() takes the region a rank has read as an
30  * FArrayBox, so the unit tests can drive it without a file.
31  */
