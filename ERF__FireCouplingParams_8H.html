Detailed Description

The inputs of the atmosphere side of the fire coupling.

The erf.fire.* keys that the shared coupling layer (FireCoupler) reads: whether the fire runs and which engine, where its grid sits, when and how its fluxes enter the atmosphere, and the smoke tracer. Every fire engine shares them; each engine reads its own keys besides. read() parses them with their range checks, aborting with a message that names the key.
