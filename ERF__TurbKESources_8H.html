Add the production and dissipation sources of the subgrid TKE (Deardorff or k-eqn RANS) or of the MYNN QKE to the RhoKE RHS.

These sources consume the face fluxes written by the scalar diffusion of the same RK stage, so they must be called after every DiffusionSrcForState_* call of the stage has returned: the RhoTheta call in erf_slow_rhs_pre (hfx_z) and the RhoQ1 call in erf_slow_rhs_post (qfx1_z).
