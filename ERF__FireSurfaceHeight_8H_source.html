19 /**
20  * @brief Fill the ground elevation of each fire cell's atmospheric column
21  *
22  * The fire module extracts wind at a height above ground by walking the
23  * atmospheric column's z_phys_cc profile, so the ground it measures from has to
24  * be the ground of that same column: the average of the four k = 0 nodes of
25  * atmospheric cell (i_f/C, j_f/C). Taking the first cell centre instead puts the
26  * extraction height half an atmospheric cell too high, and by an amount that
27  * changes with vertical resolution.
28  *

50  * Bilinear wind interpolation blends the four atmospheric columns surrounding a
