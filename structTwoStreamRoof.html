Detailed Description

A roof as the lower boundary of a column (erf.ibseb.two_stream_roofs): the column under a building starts at the first cell above the building, on a grey, diffuse surface with the roof face's own albedo, emissivity and skin temperature, in place of the ground under the building seen through the building's cells.
