Advance the fire over one step of the fire grid's level (forcing.dt from forcing.time). forcing holds the face velocities, the cell-centre heights, the lowest cell's temperature and humidity, and the surface rain if wants_surface_precip() is true; sample them onto the fire grid here and keep no pointer to them after this call.

◆ fill_fluxes()

void FireEngineTemplate::fill_fluxes ( FireFluxes &  fluxes)
overridevirtual

Point fluxes.sensible (and fluxes.latent, if the fuel releases water) at the heat flux into the air on the fire grid [W/m2], positive upward, averaged over exactly [forcing.time, forcing.time + forcing.dt] of the last advance() (sum over any substeps, divided by forcing.dt); leave a pointer nullptr for none. A vapour mass flux becomes a latent flux by multiplying by L_v (ERF_Constants.H). The fields must stay valid until the next advance(). Called only when the coupling injects heat or smoke.

Implements FireEngine.

const amrex::MultiFab* FireEngineTemplate::plot_var ( const std::string &  name) const
overridevirtual

The field plot_var_names() lists as name, on the fire grid.

One component, on FireGrid::ba and FireGrid::dm, valid until the next advance(). The coupler averages it over each atmospheric column (the grid_ratio^2 fire cells under it) and writes it into the k = 0 layer of the ERF plotfile, zero elsewhere. nullptr writes zeros.
