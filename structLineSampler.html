Sample single cells with every conserved component, as erf.sample_point does: each point is written to its own log, one row per sample, the time and then each component. A point given by a cell (erf.sample_point) is that cell of level 0. A point given by real coordinates (erf.sample_point_real, or erf.sample_point_latlon once placed) is a line of one cell, sampled on the finest level that holds it; on a stretched or terrain-fitted mesh its cell is found from the heights of its column, and with z_agl its z is the height above the ground under it.
