Interpolate the coarse field src (one component) into the fine field dst with LandWaterConsLinear. The land/water type of each coarse cell is component 0 of indicator, one value per type, on any BoxArray (it is copied onto src's cells, ghost cells included). Coarse cells it does not reach, and cells outside the domain, have no type and are never anyone's neighbour. Fine cells the interpolation does not fill (ghost cells outside the domain) keep their values in dst.
