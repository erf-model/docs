18 /**
19  * Sample the velocity at a set of points from the face-centred velocity fields.
20  *
21  * Each component is interpolated trilinearly from its own staggered grid: u from the x faces,
22  * v from the y faces, w from the z faces, so a field linear in x, y and z is reproduced
23  * exactly. The vertical weight uses the physical face heights (z_phys_nd on a stretched or
24  * terrain-following mesh, prob_lo + k dz otherwise), so the same holds on those meshes as long
25  * as the field is linear in the physical height.
26  *
27  * Every rank interpolates the points whose containing cell lies in one of its valid boxes,
28  * reading the neighbours from the ghost cells, and the results are summed across ranks; the
29  * velocity fields must therefore have at least one filled ghost cell, and z_phys_nd one ghost
30  * node, on the velocities' boxes and distribution. The ghost cells across the domain's lateral
31  * boundaries are read too (a point within half a cell of a non-periodic face takes up to half
32  * its value from them), so the fields must be filled there as well (FillPatch, not only
33  * FillBoundary). Every rank must pass the same points: the reduction is collective and the
34  * call stops when the ranks disagree on their number. In a periodic direction a point is moved by
35  * whole periods into the domain; a point on the domain's upper x, y or z face belongs to the last
36  * cell. A point outside the domain otherwise, or one found by no rank or by more than one, aborts
37  * with its coordinates: the caller owns the points and must keep them inside the domain.
38  *
39  * @param[in] U,V,W face-centred velocities on one level, ghost cells filled (m/s)
40  * @param[in] z_phys_nd nodal physical heights (m), or nullptr on a uniform-dz mesh
41  * @param[in] geom the level's geometry
42  * @param[in] pos 3*n point coordinates (x0,y0,z0, x1,y1,z1, ...) (m, ERF domain coordinates)
43  * @param[out] vel 3*n sampled velocities (m/s), the same on every rank on return
44  */
45 /// Abort, naming who, unless every rank passes the same number of points (one collective).
46 void check_same_points_everywhere (int npts, const char* who);

95 /**
96  * Return whether every point, with the cells within `reach` metres around it, lies inside the
97  * union of boxes of a level (the anchor level's grids): the sampler needs the neighbouring cells
98  * and the spreader the kernel's reach, and on a refined level that is not the whole domain. In a
99  * periodic direction the needed cells wrap across the seam to the domain's other side; in a
100  * non-periodic one they are clamped to the domain. Returns false and names the first point
101  * outside in `first_outside`. On a terrain-following or stretched mesh a point's k is not its
102  * height over dz, so z is checked as `z` says: Reach (the cells within reach of the height over dz,
103  * for a flat, uniform mesh), Footprint (the reach across only, at any height: the sampler then finds
104  * a point above or below the grids by itself), or Column (for the spreading, whose kernel would
105  * otherwise be cut where the grids stop: the reach across over every cell the heights within reach
106  * may lie in, from the mesh's bounds `zb` (mesh_z_bounds), or over the whole height without them).
107  * Not with a periodic z.
108  */
109 enum class CoverZ { Reach, Footprint, Column };
