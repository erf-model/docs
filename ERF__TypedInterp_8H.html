Interpolate the coarse fields src into the fine fields dst (same number of components) with TypedConsLinear. The type of each coarse cell is given by indicator, one value per type, on any BoxArray (it is copied onto src's cells, ghost cells included): with one component it applies to every field, with src.nComp() components component n applies to field n. Coarse cells it does not reach, and cells outside the domain, have no type and are never anyone's neighbour. Fine cells the interpolation does not fill (ghost cells outside the domain) keep their values in dst. Every field takes the boundary condition bcs[bcscomp].

inline

TypedConsLinear on data already packed for it. Components 0 to nfield-1 of csrc are the coarse fields and the nind (1 or nfield) after them their type indicators, one value per type; ghost cells included, since the interpolation reads them. The indicators are set to NaN (no type) outside the domain here. fdst has the same layout: its fields are interpolated, and keep their values in the cells the interpolation does not fill (ghost cells outside the domain); its indicator components are scratch. Every field takes the boundary condition bc.

Packing many fields moves them in one ParallelCopy rather than one apiece; a caller that packs them anyway saves interp_by_type's copies by building csrc and fdst directly.
