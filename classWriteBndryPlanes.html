How many leading rows of a time.dat with these steps and times a run starting at start_step and start_time keeps: on a restart the rows up to and including the restart that increase in step and time and are not later than the restart; on a fresh start none.

Number of leading rows of time.dat that a run starting at step start_step keeps.

A restart replays the steps after its checkpoint, so the rows for those steps – written by a run that went on past the checkpoint – are dropped and written again. Rows also stop being kept at the first one whose step or time does not increase, or that is later than the restart, or that is at the restart step but not at the restart time: those were not written by the run being continued. A fresh start begins a new series.
