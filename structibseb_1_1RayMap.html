Detailed Description

The column tops a ray cast on one rank sees: a k-collapsed map over this rank's patch of the level (its boxes grown by the halo of IBFaceSet::build_ray_map()), with the domain and the grid it is drawn on. An entry is the domain-relative k of the highest solid cell of its column, -1 where the column is fluid; column_top() turns it into a height. The patch holds the columns of other ranks and the periodic images within the halo, so the walk needs no communication. Where wrap_x (wrap_y) is set the patch spans the whole periodic domain in x (y) and a ray that leaves it comes back in on the other side; otherwise a ray that leaves the patch has left the map: the far field is open ground (see locate()). A ray is followed for as many columns as the halo's reach holds (max_steps), which is every column a ray no shallower than the map's floor can cross before it clears the tallest column or reaches the ground; a shallower ray stops there too, so a building beyond the halo's reach never shades, whether the map wraps or not. A level ray is followed for two rounds of the map and then counts as sky.
